Stage 4 output type 3, an aporia register, written over `synthesis/ledgers/intentionalitaet-ledger.md`, rebuilt on 2026-09-16 with `scripts/synthesis_ledger.py --theme intentionalitaet`. The pass read the whole ledger in consecutive line ranges, all twenty-seven records in `synthesis/inheritance/`, `synthesis/chronology.json` for dates only, and `synthesis/gaps.json` for what was already open. It did not open `commentaries/`, `articles/`, the development narrative in `synthesis/outputs/`, `synthesis/lexicon.json`, or the extraction JSON, and it uses nothing known about Husserl or Heidegger from outside those files. The ledger's header line reads "627 units and 16 recorded absences from 33 commentaries." Written 2026-09-16.

## How to read the citations

A backticked id of the form `hua43-2/013` names one unit of the intentionality ledger, and is short for `intentionalitaet/hua43-2/013`. Each unit is one claim the extraction pass made about one section of one commentary, with the commentary's own sentence attached as its quote. An id of the form `inh/hga24/004` names one engagement in an inheritance record: a place where a commentary reports its author engaging a predecessor. A backticked locator of the form `slug#section-id` appears in this register only where an extraction pass recorded a checked absence at that section; it is never used to cite a presence. Every other reference to a text goes by unit id.

Three tiers stay apart. The primary text, Husserl's or Heidegger's, reaches this register only as the commentary mediates it, so a sentence such as: in Hua VII the object is called immanent, always means that the unit's commentary reports the text so. The second tier is the commentary's own claim, which is what a unit's claim line and quote line hold. The third tier is the argument of this register, which carries no locator and borrows no authority from the other two. Every span in straight double quotes below is copied from the ledger or from an inheritance record; where it comes from a unit's quote line it is the commentary's English, and where it comes from a claim line it is the extractor's paraphrase of the commentary. None of it is the primary text.

Primary-text citations (LU V § 11, GA 24 § 9(b), Crisis § 70) are given only as the unit gives them. Dates are the ledger's section headings, which come from `synthesis/chronology.json`; where a unit carries no stratum, as with every unit of the Fifth Investigation but one, the date is the heading's range and is said to be so.

## Entries on the dated spine

## A1 · The merely intentional object: from not at all to immanent

The Fifth Investigation raises the problem as a warding-off. At LU V § 11 the unit `hua19-1/007`, recorded as criticized, says the idiom of immanent objectivity "invites two misinterpretations: a real process between consciousness and thing, and a relation between two things really in consciousness", and `hua19-1/009` gives the commentary's rendering of what the immanent object then is: "it is thus in truth not immanent or mental at all. It is, to be sure, also not extra mentem; it is not at all." The positive doctrine put in its place is thin by design: only the intentional experience is present, and to say that it is present and to say that an object is intentionally present is the same, per `hua19-1/008`, "for the one and the other say exactly the same". The Appendix to §§ 11 and 20 adds `hua19-1/036`, on which a merely intentional object means that the intention exists while the object does not, and `hua19-1/035`, recorded as criticized, on which it is absurd to distinguish the intentional object from the real one. The problem these units leave is what the object of a non-veridical act is, once it is neither a part of the act, nor outside the mind, nor anything at all. These units carry no stratum; the ledger heading dates the record 1901 to 1913.

The ledger follows the problem across the spine without ever recording it as answered. Ideas III § 16 (1912), `hua5/014`, is the first unit to give the intentional object a being: the absurd meaning "has its actual being" within the being-category of the noema, and `hua5/013` makes the objectivity a unity-moment of the noema. Ideas I §§ 88 to 90 keep both sides at once. `hua3-p34/011` reads: "The noema is not a real thing; it is the objective sense, an irreal correlate belonging to the experience." And `hua3-p34/012`, recorded as criticized, repeats the Fifth Investigation's rejection: construing the intentional object as an immanent object or inner image "is rejected by a regress argument". The ledger dates that rejection 1913 to 1928 on a single stratum. Then Hua VII, Fifteenth Lecture (1923 to 1924), falls inside that range and readmits the word. `hua7-i/003` has the object of the intentional relation as what "is the mere intentional object, the immanent one, as Brentano too, in connection with Scholasticism, introduces it." What `hua7-i/006` now wards off is only that this immanence be real: the bearing-in-itself "cannot have the sense of a real (reell) immanence". `hua7-i/009` then calls the immanent contents of the synthesis irreal. Of the Husserl records the ledger dates between Ideas I and Hua VII (Hua XXXIII, the 1919 lectures, Hua XI, Hua XXXIX, and the Sixth Investigation on its 1921 stratum), none applies the word immanent to the object of an outer act: the 1919 lectures use it of immanent perception of one's own experiences (`nug/006`, `nug/011`), and Hua XXXIX of the sensation-datum as an immanent intentional objectuality (`hua39/008`), which is the same sense. The last unit to touch the problem is Crisis § 70 (1935), `krisis/022`, whose section heading in the ledger names paradoxes of the intentional object: "The paradox of the intentional object (not a real part of consciousness, yet inseparable from it)" is answered there, in the commentary's words, by seeing that "consciousness does not contain objects but intends them", which is the doctrine of `hua19-1/008` again. On the reception layer `kern-hk/018` reports a distinction of real from intentional immanence, and `bernet-vds/042` reads the thing as a transcendence within immanence; neither is a unit of the spine.

Grade: not taken up in the ledger. No unit records the retraction of the claim that the merely intentional object is not at all, and no unit records the readmission of the word immanent between 1913 and 1923; the thing is dropped at one end and picked up at the other, and the last unit still files it under paradoxes. The silence is bounded. The Fifth Investigation pass lists "Chapter One (ego, inner perception)" as not covered, and the Hua VII pass lists "the First Chapter" as not covered, which is where gap-017 already asks for the word to be followed. The two unread files named in the Scope, Hua XIII and Hua XXIII, are the other places it would come back. The entry is the one gap-017 posed; the current ledger does not settle it, because no dated unit between Ideas I and Hua VII has been added since that gap was opened. Its status is left to the reviewer.

An article could start from the fact that the same sentence, that consciousness intends and does not contain its object, is the ledger's answer at both ends of the spine while the vocabulary in between reverses. Whether the reversal is a change of doctrine or only of idiom is exactly what the two unread files would decide.

## A2 · The ego description cannot circumvent and the act does not contain

LU V § 12, `hua19-1/012`, recorded as criticized, poses a difficulty in its own terms. The claim reads: "When we live in the act nothing is to be noticed of the ego as relation-point", and the commentary's sentence keeps both horns: "In the description the relation to the experiencing ego is not to be circumvented; but the respective experience itself does not consist in a complexion that would contain the ego-presentation as a part-experience." An ego that description must name and that the intentional experience does not contain is a problem, not a result, and the unit puts nothing in its place. The unit carries no stratum; the heading dates the record 1901 to 1913.

The ledger then records the ego admitted into the structure of the act, and never records the admission as a change. Ideas II § 22, `hua4/012`, has "a ray of directedness that I cannot describe other than as taking its point of departure in the" ego, which is the descriptive necessity of LU V § 12 turned into a structural moment; `hua4/015` (§ 25) makes every act a polarity of ego-pole and counter-pole. That record cannot be dated inside 1912 to 1928 by section, as gap-010 found. Ideas I § 115, `hua3-i/002`, keeps the other horn alive: "act-stirrings are experienced with all their intentionalities although the ego does not live in them as an accomplishing subject". Hua VII, Fifteenth Lecture (1923 to 1924), `hua7-i/001`, states the resolution as though it were settled, and with the Fifth Investigation's own qualification: the pure I belongs to all experiences "as an unlosable and numerically identical subject-pole. But indeed not as a part", with `hua7-i/008` making the I the index of a universal synthesis. `hua39/001` (1920 to 1934) has belief radiating from the ego-pole, and the last unit, Cartesian Meditations § 30 (1929 to 1931), `hua1/019`, has the transcendental ego "only is what it is in relation to intentional objectualities". Bernet's reception units `bernet-vds/027` and `bernet-vds/028` read the pure I as source and note that "what appears is that object and not the egological origin of its appearing"; they order the reception, not the spine.

Grade: not taken up in the ledger. Between LU V § 12 and Ideas II § 22 no unit records the ego-pole being admitted, and none records LU V § 12 being withdrawn. The silence is tightly bounded: the Fifth Investigation pass lists Chapter One, on the ego and inner perception, as not covered, and gap-009 already records, from that commentary's editorial marking, that the passages it assigns to first and second edition, including what gap-009 describes as the pure-ego recantations, lie in that chapter. So the ledger is silent at the one place where the commentary is known to speak. No new gap is opened; the entry cites gap-009, whose Chapter One pass would close it.

An article could start from the phrase not as a part, which LU V § 12 and Hua VII share while affirming opposite things about the ego. What the second edition of the Fifth Investigation does to § 12 is the first thing to read.

## A3 · Drive already has directedness: no non-intentional drive-layer

Hua XLIII/2, Part III § 4 (1911), `hua43-2/013`, recorded as criticized, is an objection Husserl raises against the layering he has just built. The record's preceding units lay primary content, feeling-apprehension and value-intentionality one on another: `hua43-2/010` has the primary feeling with no intentionality undergoing a feeling-apprehension, and `hua43-2/012` has a single immanent material bearing a double intentionality through double Repräsentanten. Extended to the drive-sphere the picture fails, in the commentary's words: "here at once the objection arises that this is already intentionality: there is already a directedness-toward, the striving intention fulfills itself, finds satisfaction or dissatisfaction." The claim draws the consequence: drive seems to admit no layer of primary non-intentional drive-content. The ledger records the objection as raised and not as answered.

Following its terms forward, the ledger finds the layer the objection attacked reasserted and the objection unmentioned. Ideas II § 39, `hua4/018`, a unit whose date within 1912 to 1928 cannot be fixed, has "the sensations of the desiring and willing life play an analogous role as stuff for the intentional experiences of the feeling-sphere": a hyletic layer for desire and will, without the objection. Ideas I § 85, `hua3-p34/004` (1913 to 1928), says of the sensuous data: "These data have, in themselves, nothing of intentionality." The Bernau editors, `hua33/013` (1917 to 1918), then move striving to the other side, reporting a genesis of intentionality "from pre-egoic tendencies, strivings, and inhibitions characterizing the intentionality of a passively running primal process". The passive-synthesis record (1920 to 1926) has `husserliana-11/003`, on which "Intention as such is a striving directed toward self-givenness", and `husserliana-11/004`, the last unit to touch the terms, which separates epistemic from practical striving. Every one of these takes striving to be intentional; none asks whether a drive has a primary content that is not. The undated `eu/013`, on which every cogito is a striving, is held out and not placed. On the reception layer Henry, `henry-em/012`, says modern philosophy "gives out as a discovery the thesis that affectivity is intentional" and dismantles it, and Bernet, `bernet-vds/031`, credits Husserl against Henry with "a self-consciousness having the form of an impressional intentionality"; the reception thus carries the objection forward and answers it, without either unit touching the 1911 text.

Grade: not taken up in the ledger. The Hua XLIII/2 pass note reads "Not covered: Parts I-II and Part III §§ 2-3", and the passive-synthesis pass leaves out the "full treatment of passive and active intentional modifications"; both are places the objection would return. This is the entry gap-016 posed, and the current ledger does not settle it: no unit added since that gap was opened records a non-intentional drive-layer asserted, denied or left open. Status left to the reviewer.

An article could start from the asymmetry the ledger shows: the sensation-layer survives the objection on paper for another decade while striving is quietly moved onto the intentional side. Whether Husserl ever said which side drive belongs to is the question the two uncovered parts would answer.

## A4 · Belief springs from willing: the founding relation reversed

The 1914 course records, in one record, both a founding thesis and its reversal, and marks the analysis that would reconcile them as still to be done. Hua XXVIII, Part II § 9, `hua28/004`, states the thesis: "To every valuing act there necessarily underlie intellective, objectivating acts", a founding that belongs to the essence of the valuing act, and `hua28/005` keeps value-directedness a directedness of its own on that founding. Part III § 15 then reverses the direction for the will. `hua28/011` has the future willed certain only through the will: "the will as will-certainty sets the future in a manner that first confers on it, for consciousness, the certainty of being." And `hua28/012`, recorded as revised, names the reversal: "Instead of the willing being founded upon the belief of the future being, it is rather the belief of the future that springs forth from the willing". Part III § 17 (b), `hua28/019`, is the unit that marks the matter as open: analogues of the doxic occurrences appear before all assertions in the will-sphere, and "what their performance presupposes and newly brings in is a theme for difficult analyses of its own". The problem is therefore posed by the record itself: if the will first confers the certainty of being, what founds what between will and belief.

The ledger follows the founding thesis forward and never finds the reversal again. `hua28/024` (Part IV § 21) extends fulfilment into the will as a saturation of the intention, which presupposes the intention-fulfilment structure rather than deciding the founding. Ideas II § 7, `hua4/010`, undatable within its range, has every non-objectivating act built upon objectivating acts and itself objectivating in what it newly contributes. Ideas I § 117, `hua3-i/005` (1913 to 1928), has "all acts whatsoever, including the acts of sentiment and will, are objectivating", with `hua3-i/004` giving each act an archontic thesis. The Sixth Investigation, which the ledger places at 1921 on its second-edition stratum, has `lu6/009`: "Wish-fulfilment is founded in an identification". That is the last unit that touches the direction of founding. Crisis § 20, `krisis/001` (1935), pairs willing with a mode of certainty but says nothing of founding. On the Heidegger spine GA 17 §§ 48-49 (1923 to 1924), `ga17/005`, calls the founding of every willing and loving on a representing a methodological mistake, "analogizing the study of emotional experiences to knowing"; that Heidegger's objection and Husserl's own reversal are the same problem is this register's argument, since no unit or inheritance record links them, and the entry stays open. Bernet, `bernet-vds/030`, locates the motive for aligning feelings on objectivating acts in the founding of ethics, which is reception and is not projected back.

Grade: not taken up in the ledger. Nothing after 1914 records the will-founded belief of Part III § 15 as kept, generalized or withdrawn. The Hua XXVIII pass note bounds the silence: "Not covered: formal axiology and formal Praktik proper (motivation-laws, value-comparison, the will-laws of choice and the absorption-law), the parallelism-of-logic-and-ethics argument, and, of the 1911 course of Part B, everything but Einleitung § 8". Both Ideas I passes leave out "the phenomenology-of-reason chapter (SS 136-147)", where a rational justification of the founded theses would sit. This entry is distinct from gap-010, which asks how the three statements of the objectivating thesis are ordered; the ordering bears on it, since if Ideas I § 117 is fixed at 1913 the reversal of 1914 comes after the universal thesis, but the question here is the direction of founding, not the date. It gets gap-018.

An article could start from a single course that founds valuing on objectivating acts in Part II and derives belief from willing in Part III. The word creative in the ledger's rendering of the will is where the two halves would have to be reconciled.

## A5 · The lowest layer: intentional without acts, then perhaps not intentional

The problem is first named as a problem by the Bernau editors, and the thesis it bears on is six years older. Hua XLIII/2, Part III § 4 (1911), `hua43-2/009`, has: "The deepest-lying intentionality lies in the inner consciousness that constitutes the unity in the immanent time-flux." And § 1, `hua43-2/005`, has that constitution run in the layer of mere affections, "the constituting taking place through intentionality yet not through acts proper". An intentionality that is not act-intentionality is asserted there, and not characterized. The Hua XXXIII record (1917 to 1918) names the difficulty: `hua33/002` reads "Problems arose especially from applying the schema of intentional apprehension of a sensation-content" to time-consciousness, and `hua33/009`, recorded as presupposed, puts it as two questions: "Is it an intentional, transcendentally constituting consciousness? Does its intentionality still have the form of intentional apprehension of a pre-intentional content?"

The ledger follows the lowest layer and records it changing direction. Between the two records sit the two Ideas I absences at §§ 82 and 83 (1913 to 1928), `husserliana-3-ideas-i-parts-3-4-commentary#h3-s-82-continuation-the-threefold-experience-horizon-at-the-same-time-as-the-horiz` and `husserliana-3-ideas-i-parts-3-4-commentary#h3-s-83-grasping-the-unitary-experience-stream-as-an-idea`: the stream's time-structure is analysed without the theme, and the second absence note reads the omission as being "with intentionality deferred to the next section". In the Bernau record `hua33/004`, recorded as revised, has Husserl no longer taking for granted that the primal process has the form of an objectifying intentional consciousness; `hua33/007` has it possibly pre-intentional; `hua33/010`, recorded as criticized, has its intentional character become questionable if a later egoic grasping is required; `hua33/011` and `hua33/012` set the double intentionality of the retentional flow against this. `hua33/006`, recorded as criticized, is the unit that records the outcome: the doubts pressed again in later texts, "confirming that the Bernau manuscripts reached no definitive determination of the primal process/events relation". Later spine units touch the layer without deciding it. Hua XXXIX (1920 to 1934) has the sensation-datum itself an intentional objectuality, constituted in synthesis (`hua39/008`, `hua39/015`), and `hua39/009` has "a step-wise functioning of objectualities as relative hyle for other objectualities", which names no bottom. Cartesian Meditations § 18 (1929 to 1931), `hua1/009`, has identification confront us first as "an all-pervasive, passively proceeding synthesis in the form of the continuous inner time-consciousness", a synthesis, and synthesis is per `hua1/007` the mode of connection proper to consciousness, but whether that lowest synthesis is intentional in the sense the Bernau questions ask is not said. The last unit by date range is FTL, Appendix II § 2 (c) (1929 to 1937), `ftl/024`, the retentional background as a limit-mode of consciousness. On the reception layer Bernet reads the absolute flux as "no longer, properly speaking, an intentional consciousness" (`bernet-vds/049`) and as a double impressional intentionality (`bernet-vds/034`), and Kern has the primal hyle "ultimately a mere limiting concept" (`kern-hk/009`); these are R4 and are not placed on the spine.

Grade: recorded drop. A unit, `hua33/006`, records the problem left without a determination, and two recorded absences sit in Ideas I exactly where the problem was expected. The grade is bounded twice. Every one of the fourteen Hua XXXIII units comes from the editors' introduction, and the pass note reads "Not covered: the six main text groups (Texts 1-22)"; and the editors' statement that the doubts pressed again in later time-texts is a claim about manuscripts no record in this ledger covers. This is the entry gap-011 posed. The current ledger does not settle it: the Hua XXXIII record still carries only the editors' introduction, and the later units named above touch the layer without deciding it. Status left to the reviewer.

An article could start from the three answers the ledger holds in sequence, intentional without acts, perhaps pre-intentional, passive synthesis, none of which cites the one before. The Bernau texts themselves are the only place the sequence could be shown to be one decision or three.

## A6 · What intentionality grounds in: the promise of ecstatic temporality

The Heidegger spine raises the question of intentionality's ground early and records the answer's own ground as never delivered. GA 61 (1921 to 1922), `ga61/003`, recorded as criticized, asks it in the commentary's words: "Is intentionality fallen from heaven? If it is something ultimate, in what ultimacy is it to be accepted? Not secured in a specific theoretical discoverability and experienceability." GA 17 § 50 (1923 to 1924), `ga17/006`, has intentionality "cut back to its limits, the limits of its interpretive function". GA 20 § 5 (1925), `ga20/010`, locates the dogmatism not in intentionality but in what is left beneath it "through the traditional failure to ask what it is the structure of", and `ga20/019` names the double neglect. So far the problem is posed and sharpened, not answered.

GA 24 § 9(b) (1927) gives an answer, and the treatise promises a deeper one. `hga24/019` has "intentionality is the essential, even if not the most originary, structure of the subject itself", `hga24/016` the formula "Intentionality is the ratio cognoscendi of transcendence; transcendence is the ratio essendi of intentionality in its various modes.", and `hga24/017` the intentional constitution of Dasein's comportments as the ontological condition of any transcendence. The Sein und Zeit record, which the ledger heads 1927 to 1953 and which cannot therefore be ordered against GA 24 on the ledger's evidence, has at § 69(b) `sz/021`, transcendence prior to objectification, `sz/022`, Husserl's making-present as the point of nearest approach, and `sz/023`, the note that the intentionality of consciousness grounds in Dasein's ecstatic temporality, "which the following (never published) division was to show". The inheritance record `inh/sz/004` carries the same promissory theorem. The recorded absence at § 69(c), `heidegger-sein-und-zeit-commentary#h6-c-the-temporal-problem-of-the-transcendence-of-the-world`, is the nearest place the promise could have been kept, and the word does not occur; its note draws the conclusion "so on the ledger's evidence the founding is made in the 1927 lecture course and not in the treatise". But what GA 24 § 9(b) founds intentionality in is transcendence, and no extracted unit of GA 24 says that transcendence is ecstatic temporality; the GA 24 pass note reads "the rest of the lecture course, where the theme was keyword-scanned only (§ 5 carries the only other hits)". Bernet on the reception layer supplies both the ordered circle (`bernet-vds/004`) and the remark he reports of Heidegger, that as regards time everything in Husserl remains unchanged (`bernet-vds/054`), and Franck reports the same note (`inh/franck/010`); neither is placed on the spine.

Grade: recorded drop. A unit records the division never published, and a recorded absence sits at the section where the promise would have been redeemed. What the ledger cannot show is whether the temporal grounding, as distinct from the grounding in transcendence, is made anywhere in the corpus: the GA 20 pass note reads "Not covered: the Main Part on the time-phenomenon", the Sein und Zeit pass note reads "Not covered: the temporality, death, conscience, and historicity analyses.", and the second Being and Time commentary left § 69 unrecorded because, per its pass note, the section "is left unrecorded because its 180 words are too compressed for the absence to mean anything". That is gap-019.

An article could start from the gap between two grounds, transcendence in the lecture course and ecstatic temporality in the note, and ask whether the word transcendence carried the temporal sense already. The 1925 course on the concept of time is where the ledger would have to look first.

## A7 · Anxiety cannot be grasped as a being-related-to-something

GA 17 § 50 (1923 to 1924), `ga17/007`, recorded as criticized, names a class of phenomena the concept of intentionality misses, and gives no account of how they are to be grasped instead. The commentary reads: "Anxiety cannot be grasped as a being-related-to-something; it is a phenomenon of Dasein itself." Joy, terror, grief and anxiety are "phenomena missed when construed as intentionality". What the section puts in place of intentionality is not an analysis of anxiety but the being whose phenomenon anxiety is: `ga17/008` has consciousness not a subject before an object but a being that in its being-placed-toward the world "co-cares for its own Dasein". The ground is named; the problem, what anxiety is a phenomenon of if not of something, is not taken further in the record.

The ledger holds no later spine unit on mood or anxiety at all. GA 20 (1925) contributes units on intentionality, categorial intuition and the critique of pure consciousness, and its pass note leaves out the Main Part; GA 24 (1927) treats perception; the two Sein und Zeit records place their units and absences at being-in-the-world, knowing, reality, truth and the theoretical change-over, and the first record's pass note lists temporality, death, conscience and historicity as not covered, with no mention of the analysis of attunement either way. The last unit to touch the problem is therefore `ga17/008`, in the same section that raised it. The reception layer knows a treatment the spine does not show: Henry, `henry-em/009`, recorded as criticized, breaks with Sein und Zeit on the equiprimordiality of Befindlichkeit and Verstehen, because, in the claim's words, "affectivity has already done its work when the world rises"; and Henry's `henry-em/012` and `henry-em/013` make affectivity the non-intentional foundation of every phenomenon. That Henry's affectivity is Heidegger's anxiety is not a link any unit makes, and it is not made here.

Grade: not taken up in the ledger. The ledger is simply silent after 1923 to 1924, and no pass note names the place where the problem would return, which is a stronger silence than a bounded one: the Sein und Zeit commentary's sections on mood and anxiety are neither covered nor listed as uncovered. It gets gap-020.

An article could start from a phenomenon that the course says is of Dasein itself and not of something, set against the same course's insistence that Dasein is placed toward a world. Whether anxiety has a Worauf of its own is the question the treatise's chapter on attunement would answer or refuse.

## A8 · The unity of intentio and intentum, still dark

GA 24 § 9(b) (1927), `hga24/005`, states the structure and marks its centre as unresolved: intentionality embraces intentio and intentum "in their unity, which is still dark". The inheritance record `inh/hga24/004`, recorded as a polemical rejection of Husserl, says the same of the phenomenon as a whole: "this enigmatic phenomenon of intentionality is far from being philosophically adequately grasped". The 1925 course had already made the belonging-together the basic constitution, `ga20/007`, without calling it dark. So the problem is posed by the unit itself: the two moments are distinguished, the difference of either constitutes the modes of comportment, and what makes them one is not said.

The ledger follows it within the same record and no further. § 9(c) adds a third moment, `hga24/025`, the understanding of the mode of being of what is intended, and `hga24/027`, the last unit, ties the possibility of the ontological difference to the investigation of intentionality; neither returns to the unity. On the spine nothing later takes it up: the Sein und Zeit records carry the theme out in Heidegger's own terms, and the recorded absences at § 12 in both commentaries, `heidegger-sein-und-zeit-commentary#h5-s-12-the-predelineation-of-being-in-the-world-from-the-orientation-toward-being` and `heidegger-being-and-time-commentary#h3-s-12-the-preliminary-sketch-of-being-in-the-world-from-the-orientation-toward-be`, show being-in-the-world minted without the vocabulary of intentio and intentum. Those absences sit at a record the ledger heads 1927 to 1953 and cannot be read as later than GA 24. Bernet on the reception layer names the same point the enigma: "The enigma is not transitivity but what Husserl calls the correlation of intentio and intentum, which Heidegger in turn calls their co-belonging" (`bernet-vds/002`).

Grade: not taken up in the ledger. The GA 24 pass note bounds it exactly: beyond § 9(b) and the core of § 9(c) "the rest of the lecture course" was "keyword-scanned only (§ 5 carries the only other hits)", so the word returns in that commentary only at § 5, which was not extracted, and whether the unity is named under another word is unchecked. The GA 20 pass leaves the Main Part uncovered. It gets gap-021.

An article could start from the word dark, applied by the course to the one thing its whole analysis of perception presupposes. The candidate for what lights it, the understanding of being added as a third moment, is in the very next section and is never said to do so.

## Reception-layer aporiai

## R1 · Husserl's noetics as constructive as Natorp's

Kern (1964) raises a problem about Husserl's method that his own record does not resolve. Against Kant, `kern-hk/013`, recorded as criticized, has it that "Kant's transcendental philosophy constructs hypothetical explanatory principles rather than demonstrating them in intuitive evidence.", and `kern-hk/012` gives the standard, the principle of all principles, as "refusing to go behind or beyond what intuition delivers". Against Natorp, `kern-hk/016`, recorded as criticized, has Natorp rejecting the distinction of act and content and Husserl finding in him no special noetics. Then `kern-hk/017` turns the charge round: Husserl's actually practised consciousness-research corresponds to a large extent to Natorp's position, and "the constructive character of Husserl's own noetics is acknowledged", with Natorp's influence on the noema and the ego-pole conceded. The problem Kern leaves standing is how a noetics that is constructive by its own admission meets the standard by which Kant was found wanting.

Within the Kern record nothing returns to it; `kern-hk/018`, on real and intentional immanence, is the last unit and is on another matter. On the spine no unit says of Husserl's noetic form-doctrine that it is constructed rather than seen: Ideas I § 98, `hua3-p34/019`, distinguishes the two parallelisms, and `hua3-p34/018` gives the form-doctrines of noeses and noemata, without a word on their method; both Ideas I pass notes leave out §§ 100 to 127. The inheritance record `inh/kern-hk/004` has Natorp's metabasis argument taken over into the Prolegomena, which is a debt, not an answer.

Grade: not taken up in the ledger. The Kern pass note reads "Not covered: the historical overview, the analytic/synthetic and natura-formaliter-spectata chapters, and the detailed Natorp/Rickert critique.", which names the place Kern would take it up. It gets gap-023.

An article could start from the word constructive used twice by one commentator, once as a charge and once as an admission. The question is whether the ego-pole and the noema are seen or built, and Kern's own Natorp chapters are where he would have said which.

## R2 · The being of intentionality, asked of the Fifth Meditation

Franck (1981) poses a question of Husserl that the spine poses only of Heidegger. `franck-bodies/015` reads: "the fifth Meditation treats the objectivity of the object, thereby posing the question of the being of intentionality". The frame is set by `inh/franck/009`: "the better we read Husserl, the more we can take the measure of Heidegger", with the unthought problem of flesh as what the reading exposes, and `franck-bodies/007` has phenomenology begin with perception and give "privileged status to intuitively given physical bodies". The question is the one Bernet reports Heidegger asking, `bernet-vds/003`, and the one GA 24 answers with transcendence (A6); Franck asks it inside the Cartesian Meditations, of Husserl.

The Franck record follows it no further than `franck-bodies/014`, on which being and non-being are constituted in and through intentional life, which restates the reduction rather than answering the question. The spine's Cartesian Meditations record covers §§ 50 and 51 of the Fifth Meditation, `hua1/023` and `hua1/024`, appresentation and pairing, and its pass note leaves out "most of the Fifth Meditation's constitution of intersubjectivity"; no unit of the Husserl spine asks what the being of intentionality is.

Grade: not taken up in the ledger. The Franck pass note reads "Not fully covered: later applications in Chapters Eight-Twelve, the full development of intentionality through alterity and pairing.", which is where the flesh would be given as the answer. It gets gap-024.

An article could start from one question asked twice in the reception, once of Heidegger and once of Husserl, and answered on the spine only for the first. Whether the flesh does for Husserl what transcendence does for Heidegger is Franck's claim, and the ledger cannot yet see him make it.

## R3 · Three noemata and an amphibolic X

Bernet (1994) raises, as a problem internal to Ideas I, that its central concept is three concepts. `bernet-vds/010` states the thesis of two contexts, a theory of knowledge and a theory of signification, that determine noematic sense differently and cross in Ideas I. `bernet-vds/012`, recorded as criticized, finds a first amphiboly inside the theory-of-knowledge use, `bernet-vds/015` concludes that on Husserl's own terms the punctual givenness "cannot be anything but a mental content", and `bernet-vds/018`, recorded as criticized, sums up: "Three essentially different concepts of the noema keep crossing and even merging there". `bernet-vds/021` proposes a reading of the content-sense-object sentence, and `bernet-vds/022`, recorded as criticized, withdraws the relief: a second concept of X enters from the theory of knowledge, "an amphiboly that gravely compromises the intelligibility of Ideen I." Bernet leaves the amphiboly standing; `bernet-vds/023`, the last unit of the chapter, turns instead to the hermeneutic yield of the theory of signification.

The spine neither confirms nor dissolves the charge, but it shows one thing Bernet describes. Ideas I § 133, `hua3-i/013`, recorded as revised, has Husserl redefine sense mid-book, as the matter only, with the unity of sense and thetic character now called a proposition; that is a shift of the second concept inside the text. The determinable X is recorded once in each Ideas I pass (`hua3-i/012`, `hua3-p34/023`) as the pure object-point of the noematic sense, and `hua3-i/016` (§ 149) has the regional idea's identical X posited as being, which is the second sense Bernet names. No spine unit sets the two senses against each other. Both Ideas I passes leave out § 132 and the phenomenology of reason, §§ 136 to 147, where the X as actual object would be handled.

Grade: not taken up in the ledger. The Ideas I pass notes bound the silence, "SS 132-133" in one, "the phenomenology-of-reason chapter (SS 136-147)" and "SS 137-153" between them. It gets gap-022.

An article could start from § 133's mid-book redefinition of sense, which the spine records without comment and which is the kind of shift Bernet's three concepts predict. Whether the X of § 149 is the X of § 131 is decidable from the two uncovered ranges.

## R4 · Absolute consciousness is not absolute

Bernet (1994) draws from Husserl's own description of the flux a consequence he says Husserl would not draw. `bernet-vds/049`, recorded as revised, has the absolute consciousness in which acts are temporalized be "no longer, properly speaking, an intentional consciousness"; `bernet-vds/051` and `bernet-vds/052` give the transverse and longitudinal intentionality and the belated self-giving of the latter; `bernet-vds/053`, recorded as criticized, then reads: because there is no longitudinal without transverse intentionality, "a reversible founding relation lies at the heart of originary time", so that "absolute consciousness is not absolute", which is the consequence Husserl refused to face. `bernet-vds/058`, the last unit to touch it, places absolute consciousness at the source of the subject-object difference. Bernet's own resolution is a renaming: the double intentionality of the flux is a non-objectivating ecstatic intentionality "and therefore more properly transcendence" (`bernet-vds/055`, `bernet-vds/050`), Heidegger's word.

This is the reception form of A5. The spine can neither confirm the refusal nor the reversibility: the Hua XXXIII record is the editors' introduction only, and `hua33/006` records no determination reached. Bernet's transcendence is not projected back onto the spine, and the correspondence between his reversible founding and the Bernau questions is this register's observation, not a unit's.

Grade: not taken up in the ledger, for the reception-layer claim that Husserl refused the consequence; the recorded drop of A5 is the nearest the spine comes. The Bernet pass note leaves out the Introduction and the third and fourth chapters of Part Three, the second of which is the Derrida chapter, where a reading of the flux might continue. No new gap: the pass gap-011 asks for, over Texts 1-22 of Hua XXXIII, is the one that would test Bernet's claim, and the entry cites it.

An article could start from a commentator who resolves Husserl's undecided flux by giving it Heidegger's name. What the Bernau texts say about the founding of the two intentionalities in each other is what would tell the renaming from a reading.

## Held out: undated records

No entry. The two undated records, Erfahrung und Urteil and Picht on De anima, are held out of the sequence by the ledger and are not placed here to open or close any arc above. Neither carries a unit whose own claim poses a problem about intentionality and leaves it. The one Erfahrung und Urteil unit recorded as revised, `eu/009`, is a transformation of the sense of association, not a difficulty; `eu/008`, the concealed constituting intentionality that does not lie open to reflection, states a thesis the spine also states (`ftl/006`, `krisis/004`), and no unit marks it as a problem. The one Picht unit recorded as criticized, `picht-de-anima/008`, is Picht's polemic against modern abstraction, a foil for representation rather than an aporia about intentionality. The undated `eu/013`, on which every cogito is a striving, was noted under A3 and not placed. The dating problem that would move the Erfahrung und Urteil record onto the spine is gap-015 and is not reopened.

## What this register does not support

Four existing gaps are cited and none has its status changed. gap-017 (A1) stays open because no unit between 1913 and 1923 applies the word immanent to the intentional object; the pass it asks for, over Hua XIII and the First Chapter of the Hua VII commentary, would close it, and Hua XXIII on phantasy is the further candidate. gap-009 (A2) stays open because the Fifth Investigation's Chapter One, where the commentary marks the ego passages by edition, has no intentionality pass. gap-016 (A3) stays open because Parts I to II and Part III §§ 2 to 3 of Hua XLIII/2 are unextracted. gap-011 (A5, R4) stays open because every Hua XXXIII unit comes from the editors' introduction and Texts 1 to 22 are unread; nothing added since it opened settles it.

Seven new gaps are opened, and each names what would close it. gap-018 (A4): whether Husserl kept, generalized or withdrew the 1914 derivation of belief from willing; it needs the uncovered parts of the Hua XXVIII commentary, the Part B Schlußstück and Beilagen and Part IV beyond § 21, and Ideas I §§ 136 to 147, keyed to founding, will-certainty and belief. gap-019 (A6): whether the temporal grounding of intentionality promised at Sein und Zeit § 69(b) is made anywhere in the corpus; it needs the GA 20 Main Part on time and the temporality sections of the GA 24 commentary, as absence records where the word is missing, plus the compressed § 69 of the second Being and Time commentary. gap-020 (A7): whether anxiety is treated as having a Worauf; it needs the attunement and anxiety sections of both Sein und Zeit commentaries and the GA 20 Main Part, as extraction or absence. gap-021 (A8): whether the unity of intentio and intentum is named under any other word; it needs an absence pass over the rest of GA 24 § 9(c) and the course's recapitulation of its theses, and the GA 20 Main Part. gap-022 (R3): whether the X of the noematic sense and the X of the actual state of affairs are distinguished by Husserl; it needs Ideas I §§ 132 to 133 and 136 to 147 in the Parts 3 to 4 commentary. gap-023 (R1): whether Kern says how a constructive noetics meets the principle of all principles; it needs Kern §§ 32 to 34. gap-024 (R2): whether Franck gives the flesh as the being of intentionality; it needs Franck's Chapters Eight to Twelve and the remainder of the Fifth Meditation record.

What the register does not support at all is any claim that Husserl or Heidegger abandoned a problem. Every grade of not taken up above is a fact about the ledger's coverage, and every recorded drop is a unit's or an absence note's statement, quoted as such.
