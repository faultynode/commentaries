## Considered and dropped

- §1.14: beginning "from below" (*von unten*, src L315–L317). It is (b), but the methodological point is restated more sharply in §3 and §7.
- §4.5, §4.7: presence in the flesh (*leibhaftig da*, src L441–L444), and the image as standing there "only as it were in the flesh" (*nur gleichsam leibhaft*, L450). It is (a, d), but row 3 carries *leibhaft* in its pairing with *glaubhaft*. The editor's note at L625 points to a critical note of Husserl's on "sozusagen leibhaft" [so to speak bodily] in Beilage I.
- §7.8: the possibility of transcendent validity studied "in the sphere of the most pure immanence". It is (b), but row 4 states the problem and rows 5–6 resolve it.
- §8.9: perception that "seizes" its object, against perception that merely presents it. It is (a), but §9 fixes the terms (row 5).
- §15.8–9: Brentano's "physical phenomena" in "his brilliant psychology" (*genialen Psychologie*, src L995), renamed "physical data", which differ *toto coelo* [entirely] from consciousness. It is (c), but it is subordinate to row 10.
- §15.12–13: Husserl drops the pair *Perzeption* / *Apperzeption* and avoids "the ambiguous word apperception" altogether: "apprehension suffices, as Stumpf has long advocated" (src L1001). It is (c, d), but it is a terminological footnote to row 10.
- §18.4: full and empty intentions (*volle* / *leere Intentionen*). It is (a), but the terms are the *Logical Investigations*' and are worked out in §32 (Section III).
- §19.4–6: pre-phenomenal (pre-empirical) temporality. It is (a), but the commentary defers its working-out to the time-consciousness lectures.
- §23.2: the "pregnant sense" (*prägnanten Sinn*) of the appearing side (src L1502). It is (a), but it applies row 13 rather than adding a step.
- §25.2–6: the visual and tactile fields as the only two proper fields. It is (a, b), but its consequences are drawn in §46 and §48 (Section IV).
