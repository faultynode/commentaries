## Source caveats for Stage 2 (and for any close reading)

- **Pages.** Hua pages come from running-head comments (`<!-- PARALLELISMUS ZWISCHEN LOGIK UND ETHIK 5 -->`, `<!-- 6 GRUNDFRAGEN … -->`). Many even pages have a running head without a number (`<!-- GRUNDFRAGEN … -->`), so pages are counted from the neighbours. Two markers are wrong: src L1157 reads "p. 154" where the page is 102, and src L1484 reads "p. 111" where it is 126. The last marker is Hua 159 (src L2024). The file ends at src L2025 with the sentence the commentary's last block also ends on, so the file and the commentary stop at the same point. Whether Beilage II runs on in print toward p. 162 cannot be settled from the file.
- **Vermeinen read as Verneinen.** OCR has turned *Vermeinen* / *Vermeinung* (meaning, intending, Husserl's term for the position-taking of an act) into *Verneinen* / *Verneinung* (negating, negation) in at least src L645 (twice), L676, L930, L1211 (twice) and L1988. Context settles each case: "Der Glaube ist Verneinen des Seins" [belief is negating of being] (L1211) can only be *Vermeinen* [meaning]. The commentary reads "meaning" in every case, correctly. Genuine *Verneinung* [negation] also occurs (§17 d, L1440–1446), so a reading that turns on either word must check the print. Rows 6, 14 and 25 are affected.
- **Other OCR damage.** It is scattered but real: *Gefahren* for *Gefallen* [liking] (L480), *Mittelwohnung* / *Zweckwohnung* for *Mittelwollung* / *Zweckwollung* [means-willing / end-willing] and *Fröge* (L580), *Ackererseits* for *Andererseits* (L287), *kategorialen* for *kategorischen* [categorical] Imperativ (L1657), *Scheidin* (L480), and "die Akte des *belief*" (L619), an English word where the German has a belief-term. Margin line numbers ("20", "25", "30", "35") are spliced into the text of L287, and soft line-break hyphens survive in the hard-wrapped stretches (e.g. L715–735, L1937–1983). Editorial angle brackets survive as LaTeX `$\langle … \rangle$` in about 90 lines.
- **Headings.** Several § and subsection headings are bold text, angle-bracketed text or HTML comments rather than Markdown headings (§3 L144, §5 L399 as a comment, §6 L549, §12 L1012, §13 L1165, §17 L1302, §18 L1490, §19 L1663). They are present, so the divisions resolve.
- **Editor's matter.** No editorial introduction is in any of the three Hua XXVIII files (grep for "Melle", "Einleitung des Herausgebers" and "1911/1914" across all three found nothing). The commentary's claims about what Melle "identifies" as new in the 1914 redaction (§12(b).1, §13.1) cannot be checked in the corpus.
- **Stage 2 searched** italic markup (82 lines; italics survive, mostly for *apriori*, Latin and occasional emphasis such as *er selbst spricht* at L651), „…“ quotation marks (about 367 short quoted spans), the phrasing *heißt*, *besagt* [says, means] (14), *das Wort* / *Wort „* (4), *Terminus* / *terminologisch* (4), *Äquivokation* / *äquivok* / *zweideutig* (8), and the names of other authors (Aristoteles, Platon, Sokrates, Leibniz, Kant, Hume, Mill, Fichte, Brentano, Rickert, Windelband). The file has no Greek, and spaced type was not found (one false hit, L1419), so neither was searchable. Münsterberg, Protagoras and Gorgias were not grepped by name; they were checked through the commentary only.
- **Lexicon.** `synthesis/lexicon.json` has 311 Husserl entries. Their first hits in Part A are almost all generic (Logik, Wille, Wert, Sinn) or used without discussion. The terms this course actually introduces or reshapes (*Gemütsakt*, *Stellungnahme*, *Adiaphoron*, *Wertproduktion*, *Konvenienz*, *Absorption*, the will-modalities) are either absent from the lexicon or present only as broad entries, so criterion (a) here rests mainly on the commentary's own definitions.

Count by major division: Section I (§§1–8), 7; Section II (§§9–12), 6; Section III (§§13–17), 4; Section IV (§§18–21), 6; Beilagen I–II, 3. Total 26.

## Considered and dropped

These qualify but were dropped as weaker than the rows above, per the "strongest few" rule.

- §2.8: Vaihinger's philosophy of the "As-if" and *Begriffsmythologie* (concept-mythology), as the skeptic's position (c). It is a statement of the opponent, not a step.
- §3.4: Ovid's "video meliora proboque, deteriora sequor" [I see the better and approve it, yet follow the worse]; the empiricist can act only erroneously, not evilly (b, c). It is strong, but the course drops the consequence-argument at §3.9 in favour of self-cancellation (row 2).
- §4(b).3–4: Münsterberg's parallel to Plato rejected (c). It is a misfire that row 2 replaces.
- §4(c).11: the Protagorean "Gut ist, was einem jeden für gut erscheint" [good is what seems good to each] recast as a demand (c, d, src L388). It is the recapitulation of row 2.
- §5(d).3: the will-content "This S shall be P" as the analogue of the *Satz* [proposition] (a). Row 4 and §7 carry the act/content parallel more decisively.
