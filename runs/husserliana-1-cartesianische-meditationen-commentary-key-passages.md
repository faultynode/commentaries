## Corrections to the commentary

- §22.3: the block says the system of possible objects "furnishes practically the principle for the hand to follow". The source has the idiom "gebe praktisch das Prinzip an die Hand" [practically supplies the principle], with no hand being followed. The idea is said to provide the working principle for linking the constitutive theories. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-1-cartesianische-meditationen-und-pariser-vortrage.md:1981.
- §17 (closing): the block ends with the noetic-noematic structure of the cube, but no block covers §17's last two paragraphs. Those paragraphs extend the analysis to every mode of intuition and say that only the elucidation of synthesis makes "Franz Brentano's significant discovery, that intentionality is the descriptive basic character of *psychic phenomena*" fruitful. This is the only place the Meditations name Brentano. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-1-cartesianische-meditationen-und-pariser-vortrage.md:1822, :1824.
- §64.2: the block passes from the universal concrete ontology to "So the idea of a universal philosophy is realized". It omits the paragraph between them, which says that phenomenology excludes only naive metaphysics operating with countersensical things in themselves, "*not* … metaphysics as such", and that the problems of contingent facticity, death, fate, the sense of history and "the ethical-religious problems" arise within the monadic sphere. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-1-cartesianische-meditationen-und-pariser-vortrage.md:3444–3464.
- §1.4 (omission, edition): the source prints Husserl's marginal insertion (*Einlage*) as note [^2]. It anticipates the objection that science grows in collaboration and answers it with Descartes' "theoretical autonomy". The commentary's opening note on the typescript revisions does not mention it, and no block records it. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-1-cartesianische-meditationen-und-pariser-vortrage.md:1161.

- §7.2: the block says "it is not enough to set out of force all given sciences and to treat them as inadmissible for us". The source treats them "als für uns unzulässige Vor-Urteile" [as pre-judgments inadmissible for us]: the noun, and its hyphen, are missing, so the block loses the link to §3's "fateful prejudice". Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-1-cartesianische-meditationen-und-pariser-vortrage.md:1418. (Close-reading run, row 5.)

- §11.3: the block renders "diese *Transzendenz* irreellen Beschlossenseins" as "this transcendence of unreal containedness", and "reeller Teil" as "a real part". *Reell* (really inherent, as a component of consciousness) is not *real* (worldly, existing in nature); "irreell" means "not contained as a component", not "unreal". In English the block suggests that the world's containment lacks reality, the misreading §41 rejects. Suggested: "really inherent part" and "irreal (non-componential) containedness", with a gloss. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-1-cartesianische-meditationen-und-pariser-vortrage.md:1578. (Close-reading run, row 9.)
