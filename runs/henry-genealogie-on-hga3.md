## Pair-specific notes

### Misreading guards (Henry, *Généalogie* ch. IV, on GA 3)

1. **Henry names Heidegger once in this chapter, on Commentary R's report** (R ¶25, p. 139: space "in the world", "as Heidegger will say"). The Kantbuch cross-references at ¶2, ¶4, ¶6, ¶19, ¶29 are Commentary R's glosses. Do not write that Henry follows or inverts GA 3 as though he had said so; say "Commentary R reads this as..." and test the relation on the texts.
2. **Chapter IV is a recapitulation of EM Section II (1963).** The relation to GA 3 was run there (outputs/henry-on-hga3-comparative-commentary.md). Do not redo that; point to it only where a point carries over or Henry has changed it (credit to Kant for the passivity of ipseity; the stopgap reading of B 153-157; "true a priori").
3. **Henry's "representation" is not Kant's *Vorstellung*.** GA 3 §4, 22 (Kant A 320/B 376): *Vorstellung* is the genus of intuition and concept, so sensation is itself a *Vorstellung*. Henry's "representativity" names the ecstatic horizon of objectness. "In representation no sensation is possible" holds only on Henry's sense.
4. **"Reality".** GA 3 §18, 86-87 warns that Kant's *Realität* is *Sachheit* (whatness), not actuality (*Wirklichkeit*, *Dasein*). Henry's argument runs from "sensation designates a reality" to "reality is actuality". Check each step.
5. **The Kantbuch's nothing.** GA 3 §25, 121-123 calls the object-X "a nothing" that is nevertheless "something" (pure horizon). Henry takes the privative sense only ("it is still nothing", R ¶18).
6. **A and B editions.** Henry reads B-edition texts (B 157, B 288 f., B 422 f. note, B 428 f.). GA 3 prefers the A-edition imagination (§31, 160-171; §35, 197) and reads the B passages on the understanding's influence on inner sense as Kant's recoil (§31, 163-165). Heidegger's own self-affection passage cites B 67 f. (§34, 190-191).
7. **Same word, different sense.** *Selbstheit* (selfhood, a temporal circuit of from-itself-toward and back-to-itself, GA 3 §34, 191) and Henry's *ipséité* (ipseity, self-affection as pathos). *Selbstaffektion* and *auto-affection* likewise. Hold them apart.
8. **Chronology.** Henry (1985) could know GA 3 in the 1953 French, the 4th German edition (1973, with preface), GA 25 (1977) and GA 24 (1975). He could not know the GA 3 marginalia (1991). Mark any use of these as retrospective.

### Glossary (fixed for this run; extends the EM run's)

- Henry *représentativité*, *représentabilité*: "representativity". Henry *ecstasis*, *l'ek-stase*: "ecstasis" (Henry's term, taken from the Heidegger of *Sein und Zeit*; GA 3 §24, 119 has "ekstatisch-horizontal" once). Henry *ipséité*: "ipseity". Heidegger *Selbstheit*: "selfhood". Henry *auto-impression*, *auto-affection*: "self-impression", "auto-affection". Heidegger *Selbstaffektion*: "self-affection".
- Heidegger *Gegenstehenlassen*: "letting-stand-over-against". *Hinnahme*: "taking-in". *Dawider*: "the against". *Hin-zu ... und Zurück-auf-sich*: "toward ... and back to itself". *Sich-melden*: "announcing itself". *Entstand*: "origin-stand" (what stands as having arisen, for the *intuitus originarius*).
- Kant *Vorstellung*: "representation" (genus); Henry's *représentation* is marked where it narrows to the ecstatic sense.
- Kant *Anschauung*, *Sinnlichkeit*, *Verstand*: "intuition", "sensibility", "understanding". *Empfindung*: "sensation" (Henry's *sensation*, *impression*).
