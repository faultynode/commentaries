## Corrections to the commentary

- §18 ¶8 (§18.29, §18.31): the commentary renders Henry's "existentielle ou, pour mieux dire, existentiale" as "*existential* (or, better said, *existentiell*)". Its gloss then pairs "existential" with *existenziell* and "existentiell" with *existenzial*, and calls §4 of *Sein und Zeit* its source. The French has "existentiale" as the better word (the French rendering of *existenzial*), so in the English the two terms are swapped. Source: henry-lessence-de-la-manifestation.md:923.
- §28.8: "it is said that the field in which *Erscheinen* comes to intuit itself…" is left unattributed. Henry's note 99 gives *H* 134, Heidegger's essay on Hegel's concept of experience. Source: henry-lessence-de-la-manifestation.md:1117 and the Section II notes (n. 99).
- §33.11: the commentator is unnamed. Henry's notes 108–109 give G. Varet, *L'ontologie de Sartre* (1948), 177 and 113. Source: henry-lessence-de-la-manifestation.md:1259 and the Section II notes.
- §33.10: "it is false to say that man is never short of the world, man first" quotes Heidegger, *Ueber den Humanismus* 35 (Henry's n. 107). The target of "the extension of transcendence to the whole phenomenological field" is Merleau-Ponty (n. 106, *PhP* 192–193). The commentary names neither. Source: henry-lessence-de-la-manifestation.md:1257 and the Section II notes.
