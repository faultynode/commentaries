### Misreading guards (Henry, *L'essence de la manifestation*, on *Sein und Zeit*)

1. **Henry reads SZ as one doctrine, and as a doctrine of "the essence".** SZ is a project with an announced second half (destruction, temporality as horizon of being). Henry's text treats it as a finished "philosophy of transcendence" and reads its analytic of Dasein as method for a being-doctrine. Check each unit against SZ's own marks of provisionality (SZ 17, 436-437) before judging.
2. **Sinn von Sein is not "being" and not "the essence".** SZ's *Sinn* is the "whereupon" of projection (temporality, SZ 17-19, 324); Henry's *sens de l'être* slides to "l'être lui-même", "l'essence", "l'horizon". Mark each time which is meant. The slide is Henry's move.
3. **Horizon in SZ is time, not being.** SZ 1, 17, 365: time is the horizon of the understanding of being. Henry's "horizon phénoménologique universel" is the work of being itself. Do not read Henry's horizon back into SZ's.
4. **Seinsverständnis is understanding, not light.** SZ 4-6, 147, 212: understanding of being belongs to Dasein's constitution and is a projection; Henry's "l'être luit" and "milieu de visibilité" re-inscribe it as luminosity. Lichtung (SZ 133) is Dasein's own openness, not being's shining.
5. **Fundamental ontology is the existential analytic.** SZ 13: *Fundamentalontologie* is sought in the existential analytic of Dasein. Henry uses "ontologie fondamentale" in Prelude and §2 for universal ontology of the essence, and returns to SZ's usage in §6. Track the sense each time.
6. **Descartes in SZ is left undetermined and determined by the tradition.** SZ 24-25: the *sum* is thematically undetermined and the *res cogitans* is tacitly determined as *ens creatum*. Henry's epigraph quotes the first, §7 ¶2 "corrects" with the second. Both are SZ; the correction is SZ's own.
7. **Phenomenology in SZ is a method concept (Wie), not a doctrine of evidence.** SZ 27-39. Henry's "evidence", "intuition" and "fulfilment" are Husserl's. SZ's derivation of intuition from understanding is at SZ 147; its comparison of evidence and apodictic certainty with Dasein's mode of certainty at SZ 136, 265. Do not credit SZ with Husserl's doctrines where Henry cites Husserl.
8. **Nothing in SZ identifies being and nothing.** SZ has Nichts in anxiety (SZ 186-188) and Nichtigkeit (SZ 283-285). "Néant réel", "être = néant", "néantir" belong to *Was ist Metaphysik?* (1929) and are not SZ.
9. **Chronology.** Henry read SZ in the 1941 printing and could not know the later Heidegger beyond what he names: the *Kantbuch*, the 1929 essays, *Holzwege* (1950), *Einführung in die Metaphysik* (1953), *Der Satz vom Grund* (1957), *Zur Sache des Denkens* (1969, "Zeit und Sein"), the marginalia in the *Gesamtausgabe* copy (Hütte-Exemplar), GA 20, GA 24, GA 26 and the Marburg courses. Any use is retrospective and marked.
10. **Henry's French is his own** (see above). Rendering of *Dasein* as "réalité humaine" is an intermediary: Sartre's EN (1943) and Corbin's 1938 translation. Henry also keeps "Dasein". Track which.

11. **Henry's "apparaître" merges what SZ 28-31 separates.** (Added 2026-10-19, Section I §8.) SZ distinguishes *Phänomen* (Sichzeigen), *Erscheinung* (Sich-nicht-zeigen, a reporting by something that shows) and *Schein* (semblance), and says phenomena are never appearances (SZ 30) and that Sichzeigen is not Erscheinen (SZ 36). Henry's "apparaître / apparence / phénomène" runs them together. Check every unit for which SZ term the French stands for.
12. **SZ's *Ent-fernung* is a nearing, not a distancing.** (Added 2026-10-19, §9.) SZ 105: "Verschwindenmachen der Ferne ... Näherung"; Henry's "distance phénoménologique" quotes the "active and transitive" gloss and reads it as "ce qui éloigne". Mark any later use of Entfernung, Nähe, Ferne against SZ 105-108.
13. **Henry quotes Heidegger's other texts as SZ's doctrine.** (Added 2026-10-19.) "Wesen der Ferne" is GA 9 175 (*Vom Wesen des Grundes*); "insurmontable" distance is Dufrenne's gloss on the Kantbuch (n. 27); Fichte supplies "existence" and "aliénation". Fix the source of each quoted sentence before attributing it to SZ.

14. **Henry's SZ notes can cut the clause that qualifies or reverses the sentence.** (Added 2026-10-19, §11.) n. 58 cites SZ 132 as "accepting" consciousness as the being of the "between", where SZ calls that formula nearer to the facts and "irreführend"; n. 65 (SZ 366) drops the condition "wenn das Subjekt ontologisch als existierendes Dasein begriffen wird, dessen Sein in der Zeitlichkeit gründet"; n. 64 (SZ 209) puts "l'essence de la présence" where SZ has Dasein and care. Read every SZ sentence Henry cites in its full context.

15. **"Situation" is a homonym.** (Added 2026-10-19.) SZ's *Situation* is the Da disclosed in resoluteness (SZ 299-300), closed to the Man; Henry's *situation* (Section III) covers *Geworfenheit*, *Befindlichkeit* and Merleau-Ponty's/Sartre's *situation*. Henry also renders *Bewandtnis* as "situation" (n. 186, SZ 84) and *Unbewandtnis* as "non-situation" (n. 137, SZ 343). Treat each as a rendering that carries his thesis.
16. **Henry's "Heidegger" at §42 nn. 138-146, 157, 180, 184 is *Vom Wesen des Grundes* (GA 9 165-175; WG 98-110 in his pagination), not SZ.** Henry's n. 152 cites SZ 284 for a sentence at SZ 285, and his n. 161 "SZ, 386" is SZ 286.
17. **Truncation can reverse sense.** (Added 2026-10-19.) Henry's n. 183 quote "crée l'être-été" drops the negation of SZ 340 ("schafft *nicht* erst das Gewesen"); his French of the same sentence at §43 ¶5 has "ne crée pas seulement" and "se trouver en situation" for *Sich-befinden*. Check every ellipsis in Henry's SZ quotations against the German.

### Glossary (fixed for this run)

- Translation rule: every German or French quotation carries an English rendering in the citation parenthesis or as an apposition; single quoted words count; a quotation repeated within one paragraph relies on its first rendering there. In the output English leads; in this run file the original leads.
- Henry *étant* and Heidegger *Seiendes*: "beings", "a being". Henry *existant*: "existent" (marked; Henry uses it for *étant*, often in the sense of the ontic singular). Heidegger *Sein*: "being". Henry *l'être*, *l'essence*: "being", "the essence"; the essence is Henry's term for the being that grounds manifestation and is never SZ's.
- *Seinsfrage*: "question of being"; *Sinn von Sein* / *Seinssinn*: "meaning of being"; *Seinsverständnis*: "understanding of being"; *vorontologisch*: "pre-ontological"; *Seinsart*: "mode of being"; *ontologische Differenz*: "ontological difference".

- *Dasein*: retained. Henry *réalité humaine*: "human reality" (marked). *Existenz*: "existence"; *existenzial / existenzial*: "existential" for the structure of existence (*existenzial*), "existentiell" for the ontic self-understanding (*existenziell*); Henry's French "existentiale / existentiel" tracks the same distinction and is marked when it does not.

- Henry *horizon*, *milieu de visibilité*, *distance phénoménologique*, *monisme ontologique*, *immanence*, *révélation originaire*: Henry's terms, glossed in the unit that first meets them.
- *Phänomen*: "phenomenon"; *Sichzeigen*: "showing-itself"; *Erscheinung*: "appearance"; *Erscheinen*: "appearing"; *Schein*: "semblance"; *Sehenlassen*: "letting-see"; *Verdeckung*, *Verdecktheit*: "covering-over", "coveredness".
