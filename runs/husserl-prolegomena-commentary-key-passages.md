## Backfill conventions

- **Unit.** No segmentation list survives from the original run. The commentary's lowest headings are "Paragraph n" under each §. So "§19 ¶3.2" means the second block under the "Paragraph 3" heading of §19, and "Preface ¶5.1" the first block under the Preface's "Paragraph 5". Every non-empty paragraph counts as a block. Bullet items and `---` rules do not. A few sections have no "Paragraph" heading ("§ 30 (continuation of Sigwart's doctrine)", "§ 62 (unnumbered appendix section)"). There the unit is the block's ordinal under the § heading ("§62.2").
- **Citation.** The commentary has no page citations of its own, only its § headings. The citation column therefore gives (1) the commentary's § label, (2) the true Hua § in square brackets where the commentary's number is wrong (see Corrections), (3) A/B pages and the Hua XVIII page, all read from the source file, and (4) `src L<n>`, the line of the passage's key sentence, checked by reading it. A/B pages come from markers placed at line level (`[A 58]`, `<!-- [A VI] / [B VI] -->`, `[^A 124]`, LaTeX `\text{[A 93]}`). Where a key sentence sits on a line before the next page's marker, the page can be off by one. "~" marks a page inferred from the nearest marker. Hua pages come from the running-head comments (`<!-- DIE IDEE DER REINEN LOGIK 231 -->`), which are sparse in places. Confirm every page against the print before quoting.
- **Found by.** Each reason opens with `commentary:`, `source:` or `both:`.
- **Translations.** All English renderings of the German are my own working renderings, not Findlay's. Originals are kept only where the nomination turns on them.
- **Criterion (a).** Of the ~311 Husserl entries in `synthesis/lexicon.json`, most first hit this commentary in passing (e.g. "horizon", "constitution" in §2). The rows below come from blocks that actually introduce or define a term: *Wissenschaftslehre* (theory of science), *Evidenz* (evidence), normative science, *Sollen* (ought), logical/noetic scepticism, specific relativism, the pure categories of meaning, and the theory of manifolds (*Mannigfaltigkeitslehre*).

## Edition (criterion (e)): what the commentary reads, and what the source prints

- **The commentary follows the 1900 (A) text, or a text in 1900 orthography.** Its title says "(1900)". It signs the Preface "Prof. Dr. E. G. Husserl", which the apparatus records as A-only (src L35, footnote 3: "In A folgt" [A continues with]). Its kept originals use the old spelling: *psychologische Fundirung* (psychological foundation; Preface ¶4), *Werthhaltung* (valuation; §14 ¶4–8), *mitbetheiligt* (co-involved; §20 ¶2). Hua XVIII prints B: *Fundierung*, *Werthaltung*, *mitbeteiligt* (src L15, L843, L1160).
- **The commentary records no edition difference anywhere.** It has no block on the Foreword to the second edition (*Vorwort zur zweiten Auflage*, src L40–133; B only). So every (e) row below is found by the source (`source:` or `both:`), from the A variants in the Hua apparatus ("A: …", "In A folgt …", "Zusatz von B" [addition in B]; 37 "Zusatz von B" notes in all).
- **The apparatus is partly unusable.** Footnote anchors are often missing from the body. For example, `[^55]` (src L1726, an A variant on the principle of contradiction) has no anchor in §28. Note numbers also restart and collide (`[^185]` is defined twice, L4196 and L4259). An A variant can be tied to its sentence only where the ⌜…⌝ brackets or a nearby anchor survive.

## Source caveats for Stage 2 (and for any close reading)

- **Emphasis.** The file has italics (about 457 lines with `*…*`) and no spaced type: the search for letter-spaced words found none. The apparatus refers to spaced type more than 30 times ("In A nicht gesperrt" [not spaced in A]), so Hua's spacing has been rendered as italics, or dropped. Italic emphasis can be argued from with care. Where B adds emphasis, the A text lacks it. On the garbled pages below, the absence of italics proves nothing.
- **OCR damage, §22 (A/B 68, Hua 79), src L1274–1300.** The headings at L1274 and L1278 are fragments ("### f[A 68]", "### {[B 68]"). The calculating-machine passage has broken glyphs: "W iderspruch", "N aturgesetze rseienl:L", "m acht den U nter­" (L1288–1294). Quote around them or flag them. Do not repair them. Row 13 is affected.
- **Greek is LaTeX, partly garbled.** In §2 (L254) the μετάβασις εἰς ἄλλο γένος (metabasis eis allo genos, a passing over into another kind) is legible LaTeX. The §56 heading (L3458) has "$\tilde{v}\sigma\tau\epsilon\rho\circ\nu\ \pi\rho\delta\tau\epsilon\rho\circ\nu$" for ὕστερον πρότερον (hysteron proteron, the later put first), with wrong glyphs. Row 23 cannot quote the Greek from the file.
- **Conversion debris.** L258 is a footnote filled with the converting model's own chatter ("Footnote content would go here, but this is a typo in the prompt's reference markers… Wait…"). L538 begins with "FOOTNOTES_END". L1217 uses `[nb 1]`–`[nb 3]` anchors. L3165 has an editorial aside ("Note in source text appears to have nested footnote markers"). None of these is Husserl's text. The lines around L258 should be checked against the print for lost apparatus.
- **Section headings are inconsistent.** They appear as `##`, `###`, bold or plain text (e.g. §§4–5, 13, 17, 21–22, 25–26, 28, 30, 32 are plain or bold lines). Grep the § number, not the heading level.
- **Stage 2 searched:** italic markup; »…«, „…“ and ‚…‘ quotation marks (277 lines); hyphenated coinages; Greek (the LaTeX `\mu`, `\sigma` forms as well as Unicode); Latin set off in the text (*metabasis*, *intellectus ipse*, *toto coelo*, *modus Barbara*); French (Leibniz, §60); definitional phrasing (*heißt*, *besagt*, *bedeutet*, *der Ausdruck*, *Terminus*); and the names of other authors (Mill, Spencer, Lange, Heymans, Sigwart, Erdmann, Lipps, Höfler, Mach, Avenarius, Kant, Herbart, Lotze, Leibniz, Bolzano). It also searched the apparatus for A variants and B additions. Spaced type could not be searched (see above).
