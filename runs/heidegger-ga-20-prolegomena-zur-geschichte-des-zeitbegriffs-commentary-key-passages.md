## Corrections to the commentary

All five were applied to the commentary on 2026-10-26, on branch `claude/ga20-key-passages-backfill`. The section headings were left unchanged, because synthesis locators are built from them. The added paragraphs make the GA 20 records in `synthesis/extractions/sorge`, `zeitlichkeit` and `inheritance` stale, so they need re-extraction. No quote broke.


- §3.3, and the heading "### Chapter Five" (line 437): the block says Heidegger executed "the preparatory part and the first section of the first part (the existential analytic of Dasein, §§9–36)", and the headings place §§33–36 inside the First Section. The source has a "ZWEITER ABSCHNITT [Second Section]: Die Freilegung der Zeit selbst [The laying-free of time itself]", covering §§32–36 (contents :322; body :4993, before §32 at :5001). The editor's afterword says "only the first two sections were worked out, the second, admittedly, in very compressed form" (*nur die ersten beiden Abschnitt ausgearbeitet, der zweite Abschnitt allerdings in sehr geraffter Weise*, :5196–:5200). The First Section begins at §14 (:2725), not §9.
- §23.8: the block says that the present-at-hand is "the *founded*, de-worlded residue", and it reads as if readiness-to-hand were original. The source says readiness-to-hand is itself founded: *Solche Anwesenheit von Umweltlichem, die wir* Zuhandenheit *nennen, ist eine* fundierte Präsenz. *Sie ist nicht etwas Ursprüngliches, sondern gründet in der Präsenz dessen, was in die Sorge gestellt ist* [Such presence of the environmental, which we call readiness-to-hand, is a founded presence. It is not something original, but is grounded in the presence of what is put into care]. Bodily presence (*Leibhaftigkeit*) is founded "all the more" (:3658).
- §25.1: one block stands for all of §25 (GA 20 306–324). It does not record distance, region and orientation (*Entfernung, Gegend, Orientation*, :4022), de-severing (*Ent-fernen*, :4059), or the Leibniz example in §25c (:4154).

There Heidegger calls his own phrase "the entity that has the being of Dasein" "at bottom mistaken" (*im Grunde verfehlt*), and he moves from asking *what* Dasein is to asking *who*. §26.1 starts at subsection a) (:4190).
- §31: no block covered §31 c) care and uncoveredness (*Sorge und Entdecktheit*, :4908) or f) care and intentionality (*Sorge und Intentionalität*, :4989). §31 d) on the "fore-" of understanding (*Vorhabe, Vorsicht, Vorgriff* [fore-having, fore-sight, fore-conception], :4920) appears only in part, under §32.2.

The commentary's headings read "The exposition of the question of being" and "…(unfolding it on the guiding thread of time)". These were left alone, because renaming §14 changes its section id.

- **§31.8.** The Cura-fable block omits the closing remarks of §31e: Seneca, the double sense of *cura* [care] as concern and as devotion, and the question whether natural self-interpretation already saw care (:4985).
