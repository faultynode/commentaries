## Backfill conventions

- **Unit.** No segmentation list survives from the original run. The commentary has no § numbers of its own, so a unit is the block's ordinal under its lowest heading, labelled by that heading: "No. 1.11" is the eleventh block under the No. 1 heading; "No. 3 §4.5" the fifth under §4 of No. 3; "App. IV.5" the fifth under Appendix IV; "No. 2 Elementary Account.16" the sixteenth under the No. 2 sub-heading "An Elementary Account of Object-Pole and Ego-Pole"; "Intro I.5" the fifth under heading "I." of the Editor's Introduction. Every non-empty paragraph counts as a block; bullet items and `---` rules do not.
- **Major division.** Each numbered text (Nr.) with the Beilagen (Appendices) that follow it before the next Nr.; the Editor's Introduction is a division of its own. Some short texts get no row.
- **Found by.** Each reason opens with `commentary:`, `source:` or `both:`.
- **Citation.** Hua XIV page as the commentary gives it, then `src L<n>`, the source-file line of the passage's key sentence, read in this run.

## Source caveats for Stage 2 (and for any close reading)

- **Pagination is recoverable.** Running heads survive as HTML comments (e.g. `<!-- TEXT NR. 1 7 -->`, `<!-- 8 VORBEREITUNGEN FÜR DAS „SYSTEMATISCHE WERK" 1921/22 -->`), about one per page, so Hua XIV pages can be fixed to ±0. Some heads read `<!-- p. 26 … -->` or are bare numbers (`<!-- 10 -->`) that are margin line numbers, not pages.
- **Italics survive but sparsely** (about 470 lines with `*…*` in 12,611): *meines* (L797), *eo ipso* (L823), *intellectus agens* (L1992, L2019), *Resultat* (L2665). They can be argued from where present, but absence proves little.
- **Spaced type (Sperrdruck) is not rendered.** Kern's spaced passages appear, if at all, as short hard-wrapped lines (e.g. the compossibility principle, L842–844; the No. 4 conclusion, L2659–2671). The commentary's remarks on "spacing" or "spaced emphasis" (No. 2.2, No. 2.12) cannot be checked against the file. Spaced type was therefore not searchable.
- **Leaked margin line numbers** sit inside the text in hard-wrapped stretches ("10 faltigkeit animalischer Wesen", L2662; "15 die nicht animalische", L2669; "das 15 igeine Ich", L2527). Quote around them.
- **OCR and splice damage.** L789 has lost words ("als diesen Leib in den *Inneneinstellung* nannten"), plus "Dingem", "vonherein"; L1694 "apprallentiv" for *appräsentativ*; L1371 "Erlebsetin" for *Erlebtsein*. Do not repair; flag in any reading.

- **Footnotes** are numbered per text and often restart at `[^1]`; Kern's notes ("Anm. d. Hrsg." [editor's note]) and Husserl's marginalia share the series. A note number never resolves without its text.
- **Splice found by the close readings (2026-11-27).** L1099 (No. 2, Hua XIV 28–29): Kern's note 2 breaks off at "und das" and runs on with body text ("flektives Bewusstsein ist erforderlich, um den Pol zu erfassen …"), which continues the body's "Denn ein zweites re-" at the end of L1093. ## Considered and dropped

These qualify but were dropped as weaker than the rows above, per the "strongest few" rule.

- Intro I.7: Kern sets the communal-spirit texts beside Tönnies, Simmel and Weber (c). It is the editor's frame, and Nos. 9–10 carry the substance.
- No. 1.5: the body "in inner attitude" (*Inneneinstellung*, a). The source line is damaged (L789), and the term's work is done in Rows 2–3.
- No. 2.12 and No. 2 Personal Ego.2: the guiding question, and conversion (*Umkehr*). These are strong blocks, but Row 8 carries the individuation argument.
- App. II.7: *Erlebnis* (lived experience) as being-lived, and consciousness as the ego's "medium" (a, d). It is close to Row 11, and the source line has OCR damage ("Erlebsetin", L1371).
- No. 3 §4.3: the other as a modification (*Abwandlung*) of me (a). The commentary's link to the Fifth Meditation is its own; the term recurs as No. 35's title outside the commentary's range.
- No. 3 §3.2: the body as "what is most originally mine". The tie to *Eigenheitssphäre* (sphere of ownness) is the commentary's, not the text's.
- App. VIII.1: the late rewrite of the first sheets (e, L2376) and the thesis that the "objective world" is constituted only through empathy (b, L2208). It restates Row 4.
- No. 4.8: being as an index of real capacities ("realization"). It is a premise, and Row 16 names it.
- No. 6.7: the jellyfish and Condillac's statue as the lower limit of "experience of nature" (c). It is vivid, but it is an example, not a step.
- App. XIV.14: "Without a tendency toward truth there is no truth; without development toward knowledge there is no true being" (b, L3203). It is a strong thesis, but the block's "Hegelian" reading is the commentary's own, and nothing later in the commentary's range depends on it.
- App. XVIII.8: thought possibility against real possibility (*realiter*). Row 26 carries the modal argument.
- No. 9 §1.3: the social act requires address and its reception (a). It is a short block, and Rows 31–32 carry the definition.
- No. 9 §7.2: love against the community of love as a personality of higher order (a). Row 38 states the general thesis.
- No. 10 §2.1: whether "we" is a real subject or a *façon de parler* [a manner of speaking] (b). The question is answered in Row 38.
- No. 10 §6.2: "no mere analogy … no mere image" of communal spirit (b, L4561). It is folded into Row 38.
- App. XXV.2: the organism model of humanity, cells and organs (c). Spencer and Schäffle are the commentary's names, not the source's.

## Corrections to the commentary

- No. 3 §5.1 (63–64), and No. 3 §4.5 (the note on the founding stratum): the blocks say the foreign body is experienced "as aisthēton (sensory bodiliness)" and that the stratum is "the passively constituted aisthēton". The source has σῶμα (body), not *aisthēton*: "als σῶμα (sinnliche Leiblichkeit)" [as sōma (sensory bodiliness)], and in the note "*passiv* konstituiertes σῶμα … Erst dieses σῶμα wird … Organ" [*passively* constituted sōma … only this sōma becomes … organ]. The Greek is LaTeX in the file, so check it against print. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-14-zur-phanomenologie-der-intersubjektivitat-zweiter-teil.md:1727, :1750–1751.
- App. XIII "Animals".1 (125): the block glosses "the origin prescribes a norm of sense" as "apperceptive transfer from an original institution (Urstiftung)", with the German set in parentheses as if it were Husserl's word. The source has *der Ursprung* [the origin]: "Bei jeder Apperzeption schreibt der Ursprung eine Sinnesnorm vor" [in every apperception the origin prescribes a norm of sense]. *Urstiftung* does not occur in Appendix XIII; a whole-file grep finds it (or *urstiftend*) only elsewhere (e.g. L5028, L9605, L11606). Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-14-zur-phanomenologie-der-intersubjektivitat-zweiter-teil.md:3061.
- Intro I.5 (XXI–XXII): the block's last sentence sums up the double thesis as "no real entry of one subject into another, and yet a real connection through a common nature". The source excludes only *reell* entry (as an inherent component) and affirms a connection that is intentional, through the *realen Pole* (real poles) both streams constitute; Kern's Hua XIII quotation sets "verbunden" in quotation marks and speaks of "intentionaler Identität". "Real" for both halves erases the reell/real distinction. Kern's own quotation of No. 13 also stops before the clause defining the windows ("die Fenster sind die Einfühlungen"), which the block does not say. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-14-zur-phanomenologie-der-intersubjektivitat-zweiter-teil.md:496, :5709.
- App. I.10 (38): the block calls the passage "among the first explicit statements of the static–genetic distinction". Three pages later the appendix itself says "Ich spreche da immer von statischer und genetischer Phänomenologie. Was war da der eigentlich leitende Gesichtspunkt?" [I always speak there of static and genetic phenomenology. What was the properly guiding viewpoint?], so the pair was already in use; the appendix reflects on it and recasts it in three terms. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-14-zur-phanomenologie-der-intersubjektivitat-zweiter-teil.md:1263, :1289.
- No. 3 §5.1 (63–64): the block renders the opening as an assertion ("On this account the first body experienced as a physical thing is the foreign body"). The source is subjunctive: "Danach wäre der erste Leib … der fremde Leib" [accordingly the first body … would be the foreign body], a consequence conditional on § 4's premise, which Appendix VI later calls "unvollkommen und wohl inkorrekt". Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-14-zur-phanomenologie-der-intersubjektivitat-zweiter-teil.md:1726, :2087.
- No. 3 §8.8 (73): the block glosses *intellectus agens* as Aristotle's *nous poiētikos* (*De anima* III.5), "the separable, active mind that the body conditions but does not explain". The Aristotle reference is the commentary's (the source names no author), and "separable" contradicts the source, which says the *intellectus agens* "ist … mit daseiend mit dem Naturleib, eingeordnet der objektiven Natur" [is co-existent with the natural body, ordered into objective nature]. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-14-zur-phanomenologie-der-intersubjektivitat-zweiter-teil.md:2019.
- No. 4 Result.1 (102): the block says the result's first half (no nature that is not animal nature) "claims more than the deduction shows". It claims more than the paragraph before it (Hua XIV 101), which argues only from a plurality of subjects; but it follows from No. 4's own premise that the existent is "ein Index für konstituierende Subjekte, eine reale Möglichkeit" in actual subjects (Hua XIV 93), together with No. 3's "In der solipsistisch konstituierten Welt ist notwendig da eine Aussenwelt und ein Leib" (Hua XIV 63, n. 91). The block should say which deduction. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-14-zur-phanomenologie-der-intersubjektivitat-zweiter-teil.md:2511, :2649, :2667.
- No. 5.11 (111): the block's second sentence says the strict objectivity is "for all possible knowers". The source relates it to "das wirkliche unbeschränkte „Universum“, die Allheit aller mit dem Erkennenden in Konnex stehenden Erkenntnissubjekte überhaupt" [the actual unlimited "universe", the totality of all knowing subjects whatever that stand in connection with the knower]: actual, not possible, subjects, and only those connected with the knower (the block's first sentence has this right). The distinction also closes No. 5 rather than opening No. 6. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-14-zur-phanomenologie-der-intersubjektivitat-zweiter-teil.md:2834.
- App. XIII "Animals".3 (126): the block renders "Was ich nachverstehen, wieweit ich einfügen kann" as "How far I can understand, how far I can empathize". The verb is *einfügen* [insert], not *einfühlen* [empathize]: the very word No. 3 had refused to empathy ("Einfühlung ist nicht Einfügung", Hua XIV 63), now used approvingly. The rendering erases the link. The block also adds "similar" to "der fremde Leibkörper" [the foreign bodily thing]. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-14-zur-phanomenologie-der-intersubjektivitat-zweiter-teil.md:3069.
- App. XVII.3 (143): the block renders "so „versetze“ ich mich in jedes" as "I „transfer“ myself into each". *Versetzen* here is putting oneself into another's place, the verb Appendix XXIV nominalizes as *„Hineinversetzen“*; "transfer" suggests a carrying over of content (*Übertragung*). The block's link to the Fifth Meditation's pairing also needs a guard: Hua I §51 defines pairing as "das konfigurierte Auftreten als Paar und in weiterer Folge als Gruppe" [the configured appearance as a pair and further as a group], the figural unity this appendix denies to subjects. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-14-zur-phanomenologie-der-intersubjektivitat-zweiter-teil.md:3305.
- App. XX.10 (157): the block reads „denkt immer“ as Descartes's claim "which Leibniz defended against Locke". The source names no author; the quotation marks show a borrowed formula. Descartes's version is in the corpus (Fifth Replies, AT VII 356–357, descartes-cottingham-vol2.md:9084), but there the soul always *thinks* because it is a thinking substance, while Husserl's ego "erlebt immer" [always lives through] even when "ausser Funktion" [out of function], in dreamless sleep (Hua XIV 156). The block should mark the gloss as Husserl's loosening of the formula. The Leibniz–Locke reference cannot be checked against any corpus file. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-14-zur-phanomenologie-der-intersubjektivitat-zweiter-teil.md:3666–3667, :3655.
- App. XXII.1 and .4 (160): block .1 says that a fiction carried through completely "cannot be done"; the source only says "Ich müsste doch das Stück Fiktion konsequent „zu Ende denken”" [I would after all have to think the piece of fiction consistently "to the end"], without judging whether it can. Block .4 says the note "concedes that intersubjectivity conditions eidetic method itself"; the source is conditional and hedged twice ("wenn … und vielleicht eine wesensnotwendige … wäre … Vielleicht sind das überlegungswerte Gedanken" [if … and perhaps an essentially necessary one … would be … Perhaps these are thoughts worth considering]). Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-14-zur-phanomenologie-der-intersubjektivitat-zweiter-teil.md:3746–3747, :3755–3756.
