## Backfill conventions

- **Unit.** No segmentation list survives from the original run. The commentary has no § numbers of its own, so a unit is the block's ordinal under its lowest heading, labelled by that heading: "No. 1.11" is the eleventh block under the No. 1 heading; "No. 3 §4.5" the fifth under §4 of No. 3; "App. IV.5" the fifth under Appendix IV; "No. 2 Elementary Account.16" the sixteenth under the No. 2 sub-heading "An Elementary Account of Object-Pole and Ego-Pole"; "Intro I.5" the fifth under heading "I." of the Editor's Introduction. Every non-empty paragraph counts as a block; bullet items and `---` rules do not.
- **Major division.** Each numbered text (Nr.) with the Beilagen (Appendices) that follow it before the next Nr.; the Editor's Introduction is a division of its own. Some short texts get no row.
- **Found by.** Each reason opens with `commentary:`, `source:` or `both:`.
- **Citation.** Hua XIV page as the commentary gives it, then `src L<n>`, the source-file line of the passage's key sentence, read in this run.

## Source caveats for Stage 2 (and for any close reading)

- **Pagination is recoverable.** Running heads survive as HTML comments (e.g. `<!-- TEXT NR. 1 7 -->`, `<!-- 8 VORBEREITUNGEN FÜR DAS „SYSTEMATISCHE WERK" 1921/22 -->`), about one per page, so Hua XIV pages can be fixed to ±0. Some heads read `<!-- p. 26 … -->` or are bare numbers (`<!-- 10 -->`) that are margin line numbers, not pages.
- **Italics survive but sparsely** (about 470 lines with `*…*` in 12,611): *meines* (L797), *eo ipso* (L823), *intellectus agens* (L1992, L2019), *Resultat* (L2665). They can be argued from where present, but absence proves little.
- **Spaced type (Sperrdruck) is not rendered.** Kern's spaced passages appear, if at all, as short hard-wrapped lines (e.g. the compossibility principle, L842–844; the No. 4 conclusion, L2659–2671). The commentary's remarks on "spacing" or "spaced emphasis" (No. 2.2, No. 2.12) cannot be checked against the file. Spaced type was therefore not searchable.
- **Leaked margin line numbers** sit inside the text in hard-wrapped stretches ("10 faltigkeit animalischer Wesen", L2662; "15 die nicht animalische", L2669; "das 15 igeine Ich", L2527). Quote around them.
- **OCR and splice damage.** L789 has lost words ("als diesen Leib in den *Inneneinstellung* nannten"), plus "Dingem", "vonherein"; L1694 "apprallentiv" for *appräsentativ*; L1371 "Erlebsetin" for *Erlebtsein*. Do not repair; flag in any reading.
- **Greek is LaTeX** (`$\sigma\tilde{\omega}\mu\alpha$`, L1727, L1750–1751; `$\alpha \beta \gamma$`, L1051). It is legible as σῶμα, but treat it as converted, not checked against print.
- **Footnotes** are numbered per text and often restart at `[^1]`; Kern's notes ("Anm. d. Hrsg." [editor's note]) and Husserl's marginalia share the series. A note number never resolves without its text.
- **Stage 2 searched** italic markup; „…" and ,,…'' quoted single terms; hyphenated coinages; Greek and Latin (*solus ipse*, *intellectus agens*, *eo ipso*, *causa sui*); definitional phrasing *heisst* [means], *besagt* [says], *das Wort* [the word], *bedeutet* [means]; marginal-correction phrasing (*später*, *Trugschluss* [fallacy], *unvollkommen* [imperfect], *Anm. d. ## Considered and dropped

These qualify but were dropped as weaker than the rows above, per the "strongest few" rule.

- Intro I.7: Kern sets the communal-spirit texts beside Tönnies, Simmel and Weber (c). It is the editor's frame, and Nos. 9–10 carry the substance.
- No. 1.5: the body "in inner attitude" (*Inneneinstellung*, a). The source line is damaged (L789), and the term's work is done in Rows 2–3.
- No. 2.12 and No. 2 Personal Ego.2: the guiding question, and conversion (*Umkehr*). These are strong blocks, but Row 8 carries the individuation argument.
- App. II.7: *Erlebnis* (lived experience) as being-lived, and consciousness as the ego's "medium" (a, d). It is close to Row 11, and the source line has OCR damage ("Erlebsetin", L1371).
- No. 3 §4.3: the other as a modification (*Abwandlung*) of me (a). The commentary's link to the Fifth Meditation is its own; the term recurs as No. 35's title outside the commentary's range.
- No. 3 §3.2: the body as "what is most originally mine". The tie to *Eigenheitssphäre* (sphere of ownness) is the commentary's, not the text's.
- App. VIII.1: the late rewrite of the first sheets (e, L2376) and the thesis that the "objective world" is constituted only through empathy (b, L2208). It restates Row 4.
- No. 4.8: being as an index of real capacities ("realization"). It is a premise, and Row 16 names it.
- No. 6.7: the jellyfish and Condillac's statue as the lower limit of "experience of nature" (c). It is vivid, but it is an example, not a step.
- App. XIV.14: "Without a tendency toward truth there is no truth; without development toward knowledge there is no true being" (b, L3203). It is a strong thesis, but the block's "Hegelian" reading is the commentary's own, and nothing later in the commentary's range depends on it.
- App. XVIII.8: thought possibility against real possibility (*realiter*). Row 26 carries the modal argument.
- No. 9 §1.3: the social act requires address and its reception (a). It is a short block, and Rows 31–32 carry the definition.
- No. 9 §7.2: love against the community of love as a personality of higher order (a). Row 38 states the general thesis.
- No. 10 §2.1: whether "we" is a real subject or a *façon de parler* [a manner of speaking] (b). The question is answered in Row 38.
- No. 10 §6.2: "no mere analogy … no mere image" of communal spirit (b, L4561). It is folded into Row 38.
- App. XXV.2: the organism model of humanity, cells and organs (c). Spencer and Schäffle are the commentary's names, not the source's.

## Corrections to the commentary

- No. 3 §5.1 (63–64), and No. 3 §4.5 (the note on the founding stratum): the blocks say the foreign body is experienced "as aisthēton (sensory bodiliness)" and that the stratum is "the passively constituted aisthēton". The source has σῶμα (body), not *aisthēton*: "als σῶμα (sinnliche Leiblichkeit)" [as sōma (sensory bodiliness)], and in the note "*passiv* konstituiertes σῶμα … Erst dieses σῶμα wird … Organ" [*passively* constituted sōma … only this sōma becomes … organ]. The Greek is LaTeX in the file, so check it against print. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-14-zur-phanomenologie-der-intersubjektivitat-zweiter-teil.md:1727, :1750–1751.
- App. XIII "Animals".1 (125): the block glosses "the origin prescribes a norm of sense" as "apperceptive transfer from an original institution (Urstiftung)", with the German set in parentheses as if it were Husserl's word. The source has *der Ursprung* [the origin]: "Bei jeder Apperzeption schreibt der Ursprung eine Sinnesnorm vor" [in every apperception the origin prescribes a norm of sense]. *Urstiftung* does not occur in Appendix XIII; a whole-file grep finds it (or *urstiftend*) only elsewhere (e.g. L5028, L9605, L11606). Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-14-zur-phanomenologie-der-intersubjektivitat-zweiter-teil.md:3061.
