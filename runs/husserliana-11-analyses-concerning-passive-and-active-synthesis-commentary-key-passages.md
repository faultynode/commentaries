## Source caveats for Stage 2 (and for any close reading)

## Corrections to the commentary

- Supp S1.6 (Supplementary Section 1, §§13–16): the block glosses "passive corroboration (Bewahrheitung) and active verification (Bewährung)". The section it summarizes is titled "Bekräftigung und Bewährung" [corroboration and verification]. In the main text, *Bewahrheitung* is paired with *Bekräftigung* ("erfüllende Bewahrheitung, Bekräftigung"), and §16.2 of the commentary renders *Bewahrheitung* as "confirmation". So "corroboration" stands for *Bekräftigung*, not *Bewahrheitung*, and the commentary gives *Bewahrheitung* two different English words. Source: husserl-hua-11-analysen-zur-passiven-synthesis.md:197, 3480, 1217.
- §35.1 (and TI B.3 (3)): the quoted phrase "knocks at the antechamber of the ego" merges two places. §35 says that the pull has reached the ego "sei es auch nur im Vorzimmer des Ich" [even if only in the antechamber of the ego] (L2359). The knocking ("bei ihm schon weckend anklopft" [already knocks at it, awakening]) is in Beilage XIX, on the foreground (L5267). Source: husserl-hua-11-analysen-zur-passiven-synthesis.md:2359, 5267.
