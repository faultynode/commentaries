## Source caveats for Stage 2 (and for any close reading)

- **Pagination.** Two systems are present, both sparse. HTML-comment running heads (`<!-- 136 ALLGEMEINE EINFÜHRUNG … -->`) carry the Hua III/1 page; bracketed numbers in the text (`[121]`, `[228]`) carry the original 1913 pagination as printed in the Hua margin. Only 87 bracketed markers fall in §§63–153 (about 200 pages), so a page given from the file is ±1 at best.
- **Two text regimes.** Most of the file is one paragraph per line. Some stretches are hard-wrapped at print-line length with line-end hyphenation (e.g. §70 at L3126–3134, §85 at L3862–3890, §94 at L4296–4312, §124 at L5520–5533). In these stretches a word can be split across lines ("Lebensele-/ment", L3132–3133), and Hua margin line numbers leak into the text ("Sei- 25 ten", L4296; "noetischen 35 Urteilsbegriffe", L4310; "nicht 25 verwechselt", L4595).
- **Italics are present but not complete.** The file has italic markup on 547 lines of Parts Three–Four (e.g. *reines* L2936, *esse* / *percipi* L4542, *hyletische* L3864), so emphasis can be argued from where it shows. The conversion is unreviewed, so the absence of italics proves nothing. Hua's spaced type mostly came through as italics; one spaced word survives as OCR spacing ("Be d e u tun g", L5527), so a regex for spaced type under-counts.
- **Greek is LaTeX garble.** The §85 title and text: `$\text{ὕ}\lambda\eta$`, `$v\lambda\eta$`, `$\mu\rho\rho\varphi\acute{\eta}$` (L3839, L3855). Quote the Greek from print, not from the file.

## Corrections to the commentary

- §145.3: the block says the feeling theory of evidence is one "which Husserl held in his early work and now rejects". The source presents the critique as confirming what "was briefly asserted at the beginning of this book against the perverse interpretations of evidence" (*was zu Anfang dieses Buches gegen die verkehrten Interpretationen der Evidenz kurz geltend gemacht wurde*). Its footnote points to §21, and §145's own later footnote points to *Logical Investigations* VI §34 (L6510). Nothing in the passage says Husserl ever held the view.
