## Backfill conventions

- **Unit.** No segmentation list survives from the original run, and the commentary has no § numbers. A unit is the block's ordinal under its lecture heading: "L8.3" is the third non-empty paragraph under the Eighth Lecture heading. Bullet items and `---` rules do not count. Part, Section and Chapter headings carry no blocks of their own.
- **Citation.** Lecture number, then Hua VII page, then `src L<n>`, the source-file line where the passage's key sentence sits. Each was checked by reading it. The commentary gives no main-text page numbers itself, although its introduction says it does. Its only page citations are to the Beilagen. So every page here comes from the source file's running-head markers, and is ±1 (see caveats).
- **Found by.** Each reason opens with `commentary:`, `source:` or `both:`.
- **Translations.** All renderings of German are my own working renderings, not a published translation. English leads and the original follows in parentheses, kept only where the nomination turns on it.

## Source caveats for Stage 2 (and for any close reading)

- **Invisible seams.** The 519 excised lines left no marker. Since the excised leaves were blank, no original text should be missing, but a seam can look like a break in the argument. The running-head sequence in the main text (src L287–3168) has no jumps that a seam would explain. Its gaps (e.g. 6→8 at L358, 42→44 at L929, 66→68 at L1319) are single dropped markers inside running text. No row's key sentence sits at a detectable discontinuity. Each row's surrounding lines were read for continuity. Where a page break falls inside a key sentence (rows 4 and 11), the sentence runs on correctly across the break.
- **Damage in row 12.** At src L1333–1334 the file reads "daß sie ein Vorgegeben-haben, ein Schlechthin-da-sein von Objekten prinzipiell **gelten** lassen darf" (that it may in principle **let** a having-pre-given, a sheer being-there of objects, **count**). The sense, and the commentary's "may principially *not let count*", require a negation the file lacks. A "nicht" (not) has probably dropped out, and the bold may be a spaced-type rendering that misplaced the emphasis. A whole-file grep for "Schlechthin-da-sein", "Vorgegeben-haben" and "gelten lassen darf", and their variants, finds the phrase only here. The Textkritische Anmerkungen (src L7057 ff.) are themselves garbled and give no reading for p. 68. Do not repair this from memory; check the print.
- **Displaced text in Lecture 26.** At src L3069 the paragraph beginning "den *Ideen* den Begriff der Phänomenologie…" interrupts the sentence on the occasionalists. That sentence breaks off at L3067 ("…ethischen Bedürfnissen nicht") and resumes at L3077 ("genugtun konnte", could [not] do justice). L3069 reads like the tail of Husserl's footnote (Beilage XVII reference at L3071), set into the body. Commentary L26.11–12 rightly treats it as a footnote, and L26.14 reads the occasionalist sentence whole. This could be a seam; quote neither passage without checking the print.
- **Page numbers are ±1.** Pages come from running-head comments (`<!-- KRITISCHE IDEENGESCHICHTE 5 -->`, `<!-- 6 ERSTE PHILOSOPHIE -->`). These sit irregularly at page tops and bottoms, and the first page of text (Hua VII 3) is preceded by a "4" marker. The `<!-- Husserliana VII n -->` markers are printer's signatures, not pages.
- **Emphasis.** Hua VII's spaced type comes through as `*italic*`, and sometimes `**bold**`, in 294 of the 2,882 main-text lines, spread across all 27 lectures (6–19 lines per lecture). Emphasis can be argued from, but not every gap is evidence: the converter may have dropped some, and no spaced type survives as letter-spaced text.
- **Mid-paragraph line breaks with hyphenation** (302 lines end in a hyphen) occur where the page image was transcribed line by line. They are not damage, but words are split across lines ("ent- / deckte", L1287–1290).
- **Greek** is rare in the main text (four lines) and is clean Unicode where present. Six lines carry LaTeX (`$2 \times 2 = 4$`, `$\langle$`), all harmless.
- **Stage 2 searched** the main text (src L287–3168) for italic and bold markup, „…“ and ,,…" quoted single terms, hyphenated coinages, Greek, Latin set off in the text, the phrasing *Wortsinn* (literal sense), *wortwörtlich* (literally), *das Wort* (the word), *besagt* (means), *Titel* (title), *Terminus* (term), and the names of other authors. Spaced type could not be searched as such (see above). The Ergänzende Texte, the Beilagen and the apparatus were not searched.

## Considered and dropped

These qualify but were dropped as weaker than the rows above, per the "strongest few" rule.

- L2.2: Socrates' "in-itself subsistence" (*Ansichbestehen*) of essences. It is (a), but row 3 carries the read-back.
- L2.20: the state as a personality of a higher order (*Personalitäten höherer Ordnung*). It is (a), but it is not a step the history depends on.
- L5.20: dogmatic against philosophical sciences ("sciences out of absolute justification", src L802). It is (a, b), but it is recapitulated in L8.10 and row 4.
- L8.9: "as soon as one identifies this science with psychology". It is (b), but row 9 already covers the chapter's verdict on psychology.
- L8.10: "the immortality of skepticism" (*Unsterblichkeit des Skeptizismus*, bold in the source, L1164) and the skeptical Hydra. It is (d), but it is a bridge, and row 10 holds the thesis.
- L9.9: the *cogito* refutes absolute negativism but not the skepsis about truths in themselves. It is (b), but it is subordinate to row 11.
- L10.13: the -isms (materialism, idealism, psychomonism) as symptoms of one transcendental confusion (*Widersinn*, countersense). It is (c), but it is a list rather than a step.
