## Backfill conventions

Bullet items and `---` rules do not count; bold lead-ins that are not headings ("**First**, …" in §136) do. The chapter headings of the Fourth Section carry blocks of their own ("Second Chapter.1").

## Source caveats for Stage 2 (and for any close reading)

- **Extent.** §§111–153 occupy src L4836–L6927; the subject index (*Sachregister*) follows at L6929.
- **Pagination.** Two systems. Hua III/1 page numbers sit in running-head comments (`<!-- ALLGEMEINE EINFÜHRUNG … 345 -->`). The 1913 first-edition pages sit in brackets (`[228]`, sometimes `<!-- [225] -->`), 41 of them in the range and unevenly spread. Husserl's own cross-references ("S. 226", "S. 286") use the 1913 pages. Confirm a page against the print before citing it in a reading.
- **Emphasis is not trustworthy.** Hua prints Husserl's emphasis in spaced type; the conversion has almost entirely dropped it. Only 21 lines in the range carry italics, nearly all Latin (*cogitationes*, *fiat*, *res extensa*, *mathesis universalis*). Spaced type survives once, as garbled letter-spacing: "Be d e u tun g" (L5527). One emphasised term of art survives as italics, *Intentionalität* (L6590). **The absence of emphasis proves nothing anywhere in this file.**
- **Quotation marks survive** (mixed `,,…''`, `„…“`, `"…"`) and are the one wording marker that can be argued from.
- **Line layout varies.** Long paragraph-lines alternate with short hyphenated print lines (e.g. L5517–5596, L6682–6744, L6900–6927), so one sentence can run over a dozen file lines. 27 lines in the range carry soft hyphens; stray OCR junk (`be-I` L5535, `[^5]` L5541, `!` L5521) sits inside sentences.
- **Demoted or broken headings.** §128 is plain text (L5682), §130 is a footnote (`[^131]:` L5834), §136 plain text (L6144), §146 bold (L6583). §143 runs inline at the end of a §142 line ("a priori vorgezeichnet. § 143. Adäquate Dinggegebenheit als 35", L6429), its title's end "Idee im Kantischen Sinne" [idea in the Kantian sense] is footnote `[^140]` (L6449), and a body line is promoted to `###` (L6431). §117's heading is truncated (L5231); §150's subtitle is split off (L6773). Heading levels mix `##` and `###`. A `## §` census under-counts.
- **Duplicate footnote labels.** Two `[^145]` at L6768–6769 (a cross-reference to §143 and Kant's fifth space argument, *KrV* A 25). A footnote marker in §149 cannot be resolved by its label alone.
- **Seam at L4995 (§114).** "…die ebenfalls zu jedem Erlebnis — als Erlebnis — [233] gegenwart im inneren Zeitbewußtsein — ein Gegenstück…" [which likewise, for every experience — as experience — [233] present in inner time-consciousness — a counterpart]: words are lost at the page marker. Kersten (cw2 L3353) has "as the present of the mental process in the consciousness…". Do not repair; quote around it.
- **OCR inverting sense at L5739 (§129).** "Ein radikaler Fortschritt war eben von einer systematischen Phänomenologie … gar nicht möglich" [a radical advance was not possible *from* a systematic phenomenology]; Kersten (cw2 L3821) reads "before a systematic phenomenology". The commentary (§129.2) has the right sense. Also "Apop hantische" for *Apophantische* [apophantic] (L6006).
- **Kersten file** is itself OCR-damaged (`037` glyph substitutions, broken words). Use it for sense and for its editorial notes, not for wording.
