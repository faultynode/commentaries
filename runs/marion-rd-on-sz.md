## Pair-specific notes
(misreading guards, glossary decisions, edition mappings, settled once and kept)

### Misreading guards (Marion, *Réduction et donation*, on *Sein und Zeit*)

1. **Sein und Zeit has no reduction.** The word "Reduktion" does not occur in SZ. Heidegger's own name for leading the gaze back from entities
   to being as "phänomenologische Reduktion" is GA 24 §5 (1927, published 1975), with the reservation that this ties into Husserl's term
   "dem Wortlaut, nicht aber der Sache nach" [in wording, not in substance] (GA 24 source :458). Marion's "Heideggerian reduction", "redoubled
   reduction" and "second reduction" are R's names for SZ's procedure. Test each against SZ's own method terms: Freilegung, Aufweisung,
   Ausweisung, Destruktion, Auslegung, phenomenology as "Methodenbegriff" (§7).
2. **Sein und Zeit treats givenness as a trap, not as its theme.** *Gegebenheit* occurs about ten times in SZ, mostly against itself: "die
   Gegebenheit des Ich" as the existential analytic's possible trap (§25, SZ 115), "die Evidenz einer unmittelbaren Gegebenheit der
   Erlebnisse, des Ich und des Bewußtseins" falling short of the certainty of anticipation (§53, SZ 265), "Gegebenheit" in scare quotes
   for Dasein's whole (§62, SZ 309). SZ's operative words are showing (§7: the phenomenon as "das Sich-an-ihm-selbst-zeigende", SZ 28),
   disclosedness and uncoveredness (§44), and once the "es gibt" of being (§44 c). Marion's thesis that Heidegger "thinks givenness" (R I.7 ¶4)
   is R's re-inscription, and must be marked where it is laid on an SZ locus.
3. **The courses are not the book.** Marion reads SZ through GA 20 (1925; published 1979), GA 24 (1927; 1975), GA 26 (1928) and GA 29/30
   (1929/30) and the Zähringen seminar (1973). He says himself that the 1925 course is "more explicit here than *Sein und Zeit*" (R II.6).
   A doctrine of a course is SZ's only where SZ states it. Name the intermediary each time (mediation), and say what SZ says at the locus.
4. **The marginalia are not the 1927 text.** R's "Ontologische Differenz" notes (chapter IV) come from Heidegger's own copy, published in GA 2,
   undated in R. They are self-interpretation of uncertain date, not available to verify in this corpus (see the edition note above).
   Never let one stand as what SZ says.
5. **SZ's "ontological difference" is the difference between kinds of being.** "den ontologischen Unterschied zwischen dem In-Sein als
   Existenzial und der »Inwendigkeit« von Vorhandenem untereinander als Kategorie" (§12, SZ 56), "eine erste ontologische *Unterscheidung* von
   Existenz und Realität" (§63, SZ 314; *Unterscheidung*, distinction, not *Unterschied*) and the "Unterschied des Seins des existierenden
   Daseins gegenüber dem Sein des nichtdaseinsmäßigen Seienden" (§83, SZ 437) separate Dasein's being (existence, existentials) from the being
   of other entities (reality, presence-at-hand, categories). The difference between being and entities ("Sein ist nicht ein Seiendes", §2,
   SZ 6; "unterschieden" at the close of Division One) is another relation. Marion's chapter IV joins the two under one name. Keep them apart
   in every unit, and mark where R moves from one to the other. SZ itself gives R a hinge: the existence/reality distinction "kann sich nur in
   *deren* [an idea of being in general] Horizont vollziehen. Beide meinen doch *Sein*" [can be drawn only in the horizon of an idea of being
   in general. Both mean *being*] (§63, SZ 314).
6. **The triad of §2 has fixed terms.** "Das Gefragte" (what is asked about) is being; "das Befragte" (what is interrogated) is the entity, and
   exemplarily Dasein; "das Erfragte" (what is to be found out) is the sense of being (SZ 5-6). Marion's police analogy and his "redoubled
   reduction" map onto these terms; check his mapping each time against SZ's, and against GA 20's version (R II.6: the 1925 course "names the
   three elements").
7. **Presence: Anwesenheit is SZ's term for ancient ontology, not a name for metaphysics as such.** SZ §6 (SZ 25-26) reads παρουσία/οὐσία as
   "Anwesenheit", being understood from the "Gegenwart" (present). "Metaphysics of presence" is Derrida's phrase, and the identification of
   metaphysics as such with the primacy of presence is later Heidegger. SZ's own critical term for the modern object is Vorhandenheit
   (presence-at-hand). Do not let "presence" in R stand for SZ's Anwesenheit without saying which.
8. **Dasein is not the subject, and SZ says why.** §10 (SZ 46) demarcates the analytic from "subject", "soul", "consciousness", "spirit",
   "person"; §25 refuses to start from the givenness of the I. When R says Dasein "remains haunted by the I" (R Phenomenology as such ¶5) or
   "confirms the ego" (chapter III), test the claim against SZ's §§10, 25, 64 (the self as care, SZ 316-323), not against Husserl's ego alone.
9. **Anxiety: 1927 and 1929 are two texts.** SZ §40 anxiety singularizes Dasein before being-in-the-world; *Was ist Metaphysik?* (1929) is not in
   the corpus. Where R reads the 1929 lecture against SZ (II.7, VI), check SZ §40 and say that the lecture is checked only as R quotes it.
10. **Marion's "possibility higher than actuality" is a tribute turned into a method.** SZ 38 sets possibility above actuality inside the
   paragraph that acknowledges Husserl's ground; its "actuality" is phenomenology's being actual as a "Richtung" [school, movement]. Marion
   adds "in phenomenology, as against metaphysics" and "in truth", and turns the sentence against Heidegger too (R Phenomenology as such ¶6).

### Glossary (fixed for this run)

- Translation rule: as in runs/marion-rd-on-hua3.md. In the output English leads and an original is kept only for glossary terms and wording
  whose sense departs from the English in a way the argument turns on; in this run file the original leads.
- Dasein (being-there), retained. *Sein*: "being"; *Seiendes*: "entity" (plural "entities"; R's *étant*); *Sein des Seienden*: "the being of
  entities"; *Sinn von Sein*: "sense of being" (R *sens de l'être*; Martineau *sens de l'être*).
- §2 triad: *das Gefragte*: "what is asked about"; *das Befragte*: "what is interrogated"; *das Erfragte*: "what is to be found out"
  (Martineau: *le questionné*, *l'interrogé*, *le demandé*).
- *Vorhandenheit* / *vorhanden*: "presence-at-hand" / "present-at-hand" (Martineau *sous-la-main*); *Zuhandenheit*: "readiness-to-hand"
  (Martineau *à-portée-de-la-main*); *Anwesenheit*: "presence" (marked whenever R's *présence* renders it); *Gegenwart*: "the present".
- *In-der-Welt-sein*: "being-in-the-world"; *Sorge*: "care"; *Angst*: "anxiety"; *Befindlichkeit*: "disposedness"; *Erschlossenheit*: "disclosedness";
  *Entdecktheit*: "uncoveredness"; *Existenz* / *existenzial*: "existence" / "existential"; *kategorial*: "categorial".
- *ontologische Differenz*: "ontological difference"; *ontologischer Unterschied*: "ontological distinction" when SZ's sense (kinds of being) is
  meant and R's translation as *différence* is at issue, otherwise "ontological difference"; *unterscheiden*: "to distinguish".
- *Bedeutsamkeit*: "significance" (the world's relational whole); *Bedeutung*: "signification"; *Geltung*: "validity";
  *Gegenständlichkeit* (R *objectité*): "objectness"; *Freilegung*: "freeing" (SZ §5 title; GA 20 "Freilegung des Bodens").
- *Gegebenheit*: "givenness" (SZ's word, usually critical); R *donation*: "givenness" (R's thesis word); *sich zeigen*: "to show itself";
  *Phänomen*: "phenomenon".
- *Destruktion*: "destruction"; *Wiederholung*: "repetition"; *Möglichkeit* / *Wirklichkeit*: "possibility" / "actuality" (Martineau
  *effectivité*, R *effectivité*).
- R *re(con)duction*: kept as R's coinage, glossed "reduction as leading back".
