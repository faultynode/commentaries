### Misreading guards (Marion, *Réduction et donation*, on *Sein und Zeit*)

1. **Sein und Zeit has no reduction.** The word "Reduktion" does not occur in SZ. Heidegger's own name for leading the gaze back from entities
   to being as "phänomenologische Reduktion" is GA 24 §5 (1927, published 1975), with the reservation that this ties into Husserl's term
   "dem Wortlaut, nicht aber der Sache nach" [in wording, not in substance] (GA 24 source :458). Marion's "Heideggerian reduction", "redoubled
   reduction" and "second reduction" are R's names for SZ's procedure. Test each against SZ's own method terms: Freilegung, Aufweisung,
   Ausweisung, Destruktion, Auslegung, phenomenology as "Methodenbegriff" (§7).
2. **Sein und Zeit treats givenness as a trap, not as its theme.** *Gegebenheit* occurs about ten times in SZ, mostly against itself: "die
   Gegebenheit des Ich" as the existential analytic's possible trap (§25, SZ 115), "die Evidenz einer unmittelbaren Gegebenheit der
   Erlebnisse, des Ich und des Bewußtseins" falling short of the certainty of anticipation (§53, SZ 265), "Gegebenheit" in scare quotes
   for Dasein's whole (§62, SZ 309). SZ's operative words are showing (§7: the phenomenon as "das Sich-an-ihm-selbst-zeigende", SZ 28),
   disclosedness and uncoveredness (§44), and once the "es gibt" of being (§44 c). Marion's thesis that Heidegger "thinks givenness" (R I.7 ¶4)
   is R's re-inscription, and must be marked where it is laid on an SZ locus.
3. **The courses are not the book.** Marion reads SZ through GA 20 (1925; published 1979), GA 24 (1927; 1975), GA 26 (1928) and GA 29/30
   (1929/30) and the Zähringen seminar (1973). He says himself that the 1925 course is "more explicit here than *Sein und Zeit*" (R II.6).
   A doctrine of a course is SZ's only where SZ states it. Name the intermediary each time (mediation), and say what SZ says at the locus.
4. **The marginalia are not the 1927 text.** R's "Ontologische Differenz" notes (chapter IV) come from Heidegger's own copy, published in GA 2,
   undated in R. They are self-interpretation of uncertain date, not available to verify in this corpus (see the edition note above).
   Never let one stand as what SZ says.
5. **SZ's "ontological difference" is the difference between kinds of being.** "den ontologischen Unterschied zwischen dem In-Sein als
   Existenzial und der »Inwendigkeit« von Vorhandenem untereinander als Kategorie" (§12, SZ 56), "eine erste ontologische *Unterscheidung* von
   Existenz und Realität" (§63, SZ 314; *Unterscheidung*, distinction, not *Unterschied*) and the "Unterschied des Seins des existierenden
   Daseins gegenüber dem Sein des nichtdaseinsmäßigen Seienden" (§83, SZ 437) separate Dasein's being (existence, existentials) from the being
   of other entities (reality, presence-at-hand, categories). The difference between being and entities ("Sein ist nicht ein Seiendes", §2,
   SZ 6; "unterschieden" at the close of Division One) is another relation. Marion's chapter IV joins the two under one name. Keep them apart
   in every unit, and mark where R moves from one to the other. SZ itself gives R a hinge: the existence/reality distinction "kann sich nur in
   *deren* [an idea of being in general] Horizont vollziehen. Beide meinen doch *Sein*" [can be drawn only in the horizon of an idea of being
   in general. Both mean *being*] (§63, SZ 314).
6. **The triad of §2 has fixed terms.** "Das Gefragte" (what is asked about) is being; "das Befragte" (what is interrogated) is the entity, and
   exemplarily Dasein; "das Erfragte" (what is to be found out) is the sense of being (SZ 5-6). Marion's police analogy and his "redoubled
   reduction" map onto these terms; check his mapping each time against SZ's, and against GA 20's version (R II.6: the 1925 course "names the
   three elements").
7. **Presence: Anwesenheit is SZ's term for ancient ontology, not a name for metaphysics as such.** SZ §6 (SZ 25-26) reads παρουσία/οὐσία as
   "Anwesenheit", being understood from the "Gegenwart" (present). "Metaphysics of presence" is Derrida's phrase, and the identification of
   metaphysics as such with the primacy of presence is later Heidegger. SZ's own critical term for the modern object is Vorhandenheit
   (presence-at-hand). Do not let "presence" in R stand for SZ's Anwesenheit without saying which.
8. **Dasein is not the subject, and SZ says why.** §10 (SZ 46) demarcates the analytic from "subject", "soul", "consciousness", "spirit",
   "person"; §25 refuses to start from the givenness of the I. When R says Dasein "remains haunted by the I" (R Phenomenology as such ¶5) or
   "confirms the ego" (chapter III), test the claim against SZ's §§10, 25, 64 (the self as care, SZ 316-323), not against Husserl's ego alone.
9. **Anxiety: 1927 and 1929 are two texts.** SZ §40 anxiety singularizes Dasein before being-in-the-world; *Was ist Metaphysik?* (1929) is not in
   the corpus. Where R reads the 1929 lecture against SZ (II.7, VI), check SZ §40 and say that the lecture is checked only as R quotes it.
10. **Marion's "possibility higher than actuality" is a tribute turned into a method.** SZ 38 sets possibility above actuality inside the
   paragraph that acknowledges Husserl's ground; its "actuality" is phenomenology's being actual as a "Richtung" [school, movement]. Marion
   adds "in phenomenology, as against metaphysics" and "in truth", and turns the sentence against Heidegger too (R Phenomenology as such ¶6).

11. **R's quotation of GA 24 §5 is cut at both ends.** (Added 2026-11-02, chapter II.) R II.5 ¶2 / n. 65 begins at "Für Husserl" and omits the
   sentences before it: the reduction as "Rückführung des untersuchenden Blicks vom naiv erfaßten Seienden zum Sein", and "Damit knüpfen wir
   dem Wortlaut, nicht aber der Sache nach an einen zentralen Terminus der Phänomenologie Husserls an". R inserts "[au contraire]". It stops
   before "nicht das einzige, ja nicht einmal das zentrale Grundstück der phänomenologischen Methode" (GA 24, 29). R's French "la saisie de
   l'étant comme toujours déterminé" renders "der wie immer bestimmten Erfassung des Seienden" [the apprehension of the entity, however
   determined]. R's three-term "redoubled reduction", with Husserl's reduction as the first stage, contradicts GA 24's starting point in the
   naively apprehended entity. Hold every later "reduction" in R to this.
12. **R turns SZ's showing into giving.** (Added 2026-11-02.) R II.4 ¶1: "Le phénomène se donne de lui-même" glosses SZ 28's "das
   Sich-an-ihm-selbst-zeigende". Martineau "ce-qui-se-montre-en-lui-même"; R "ce-qui-se-montre-en-soi-même", then "se-montrant-de-soi-même"
   (R n. 49: "à partir de soi-même", after Hyppolite). "von ihm selbst her" is SZ 34 (the formal sense of phenomenology), not SZ 28. SZ 28 adds
   "je nach der Zugangsart zu ihm". SZ's one use of "gegeben" in this sense is negative: "weil die Phänomene zunächst und zumeist *nicht*
   gegeben sind, bedarf es der Phänomenologie" (SZ 36).
13. **SZ 152 refuses "being without regard for entities".** (Added 2026-11-02.) "Der Sinn von Sein kann nie in Gegensatz gebracht werden zum
   Seienden oder zum Sein als tragenden »Grund« des Seienden" [the sense of being can never be set in opposition to entities, or to being as
   the supporting "ground" of entities]; the inquiry "fragt nach ihm selbst, sofern es in die Verständlichkeit des Daseins hereinsteht" (SZ 152).
   R II.6 takes "être sans égard pour l'étant" from *Zeit und Sein* (1962) and makes it SZ's goal. Test chapters IV and VI against SZ 151-152.
14. **SZ's answer to the sense of being is time.** (Added 2026-11-02.) SZ 17 (the horizon for the most original interpretation of being), SZ 437
   (the closing questions). R II.7 ¶2 replaces it, "as a simple hypothesis", by ontological difference. Mark every substitution.
15. **SZ's cause of the undetermined *sum* is the *ens creatum*, not certainty.** (Added 2026-11-22, chapter III.) "Übertragung der
   mittelalterlichen Ontologie auf die von ihm als fundamentum inconcussum angesetzte Seiende ... ens creatum" (§6, SZ 25). R III.3 ¶1 (b)
   substitutes the univocity of methodical certainty and the "grey ontology" of the *Regulae* (Marion's own Descartes books). R III n. 11 says
   Heidegger "passes over in silence" the finite/infinite difference; SZ 92 names it ("ein »unendlicher« Unterschied ihres Seins") and SZ 93
   makes it the problem of the analogy of being.
16. **SZ's first person is a formal indication.** (Added 2026-11-22.) "Das »Ich« darf nur verstanden werden im Sinne einer unverbindlichen
   *formalen Anzeige*" (§25, SZ 116); "Im »Ich« spricht sich das Selbst aus, das ich zunächst und zumeist *nicht* eigentlich bin" (§64, SZ 322).
   R III.6 ¶3 and III.7 ¶3 read SZ 42's "ich bin" as *ego sum* without SZ 116. Hold every "repetition of the ego" to these two pages.
17. **SZ 211 is a conditional.** (Added 2026-11-22.) "Sollte das »cogito sum« als Ausgang der existenzialen Analytik des Daseins dienen, dann
   bedarf es ..." (§43c). R III.7 ¶1 reads it as Heidegger's sketch of a retranscription. It states what the start would need and that Descartes
   did not meet it.
18. **R's "note de 1929" to GA 9, 134 is a later marginal note.** (Added 2026-11-22, chapter IV.) The corpus text heads it "1. Auflage 1929";
   the corpus map identifies these headings as marginalia in Heidegger's copy. R IV.2 ¶3 uses n. b ("die erste öffentliche Mitteilung ... SS
   1927 ... § 22") to date the first naming and does not cite nn. a and c on the same sentence, which call the distinction "eine verhängnisvolle
   Rückbindung" and say truth is here grasped "vom »Unterschied« als fester Marke her".
19. **SZ subordinates the distinction to the sense of being.** (Added 2026-11-22.) "Was es bedeutet: Sein »ist«, wo es doch von allem Seienden
   unterschieden sein soll, kann erst konkret gefragt werden, wenn der Sinn von Sein und die Tragweite von Seinsverständnis überhaupt
   aufgeklärt sind" (§44c, SZ 230). With SZ 314 ("Beide meinen doch *Sein*") and SZ 437, this is the published order R IV.5 reconstructs.
   R cites SZ 230 only as an occurrence of the difference.
20. **R's extra occurrences are a preposition and an *Unterscheidung*.** (Added 2026-11-22.) SZ 188: "im Unterschied von der kategorialen
   Bedeutung" (Martineau "par opposition à", defensible); SZ 314: *Unterscheidung* (Martineau "différenciation", then "distinction" a few lines
   later; R n. 22 rightly notes the inconsistency). Only SZ 56 joins "ontologisch" to *Unterschied*. All SZ occurrences are R's own figure β
   (n. 36). Marion's correction of Martineau at SZ 230 ("si tant est que" for an adversative "doch") holds.
21. **R V.7 ¶2 states guard 1 in its own words.** (Added 2026-11-24, chapter V.) Heidegger "prétend, du moins dans *Sein und Zeit*, assumer la
   méthode phénoménologique sans pourtant jamais faire droit à la réduction" (n. 59: "Du moins explicitement. Voir *supra*, chap. II, § 5-7").
   This stands against R II.5 ¶2 ("Heidegger reste donc phénoménologue, puisqu'il reprend la réduction"). Cite both whenever chapter VI's
   "third reduction" presupposes a second one in SZ.
22. **SZ already makes R's critique of formal ontology, uncited.** (Added 2026-11-24.) Formalizations "die Phänomene so weit nivellieren, daß der
   eigentliche phänomenale Gehalt verloren geht" (§18, SZ 88); existential possibility "unterscheidet sich ebensosehr von der leeren, logischen
   Möglichkeit wie von der Kontingenz eines Vorhandenen" (§31, SZ 143); traditional logic "selbst ihre Fundamente in der antiken Ontologie hat"
   (§1, SZ 4); "Alle Ontologie ... bleibt im Grunde blind", without the sense of being (§3, SZ 11). SZ 77 n. cites Ideen I §10 ff. and LU I ch. 11
   for formal ontology, against R V.3 ¶3's "Heidegger ... toujours exclusivement ... la *Recherche VI*".
23. **SZ fills the formula of the call, and counts not hearing as a mode of being.** (Added 2026-11-24, chapter VI.) "Ontologisch genügt
   jedoch die Antwort, das Dasein sei der Rufer und der Angerufene *zumal*, keineswegs" (SZ 275); "Der Satz ... hat jetzt seine formale Leere
   ... verloren ... Der Angerufene ist eben dieses Dasein, aufgerufen zu seinem eigensten Seinkönnen" (SZ 277-278); "in jedem Überhören des
   Rufes ... eine *bestimmte Seinsart* des Daseins. Ein freischwebender Ruf, auf den »nichts erfolgt«, ist, existenzial gesehen, eine unmögliche
   Fiktion" (SZ 279). R VI.5 ¶2 quotes "Streng genommen - nichts" (SZ 273) and the formula, not their filling; R's "contre-existential" and
   "forme pure de l'appel" must meet SZ 279.
24. **SZ's "Da" is disclosedness, not a site prior to being.** (Added 2026-11-24.) "Das Dasein *ist* seine Erschlossenheit" (SZ 133);
   "»Hier« und »Dort« sind nur möglich in einem »Da«" (SZ 132). R VI.6 ¶4 and VI.7 ¶3 ("le *là* y précède et détermine toujours l'être — *da
   sein*") contradict it.
25. **R tests the later notes in chapter VI and not in chapter IV.** (Added 2026-11-24.) R VI.3 ¶5-¶6 treats the 1949 notes to *Was ist
   Metaphysik?* as "procédures tardives et laborieuses ... après coup"; R IV.3 ¶5 and IV.7 treat the notes in Heidegger's copy of SZ as
   confirmation and answer. R n. 51 says Heidegger added ontological difference to the 1929 lecture in 1949 while conceding the body lacked it.

### Glossary (fixed for this run)

- Translation rule: as in runs/marion-rd-on-hua3.md. In the output English leads and an original is kept only for glossary terms and wording
  whose sense departs from the English in a way the argument turns on; in this run file the original leads.
- Dasein (being-there), retained. *Sein*: "being"; *Seiendes*: "entity" (plural "entities"; R's *étant*); *Sein des Seienden*: "the being of
  entities"; *Sinn von Sein*: "sense of being" (R *sens de l'être*; Martineau *sens de l'être*).
- §2 triad: *das Gefragte*: "what is asked about"; *das Befragte*: "what is interrogated"; *das Erfragte*: "what is to be found out"
  (Martineau: *le questionné*, *l'interrogé*, *le demandé*).

  (Martineau *à-portée-de-la-main*); *Anwesenheit*: "presence" (marked whenever R's *présence* renders it); *Gegenwart*: "the present".
- *In-der-Welt-sein*: "being-in-the-world"; *Sorge*: "care"; *Angst*: "anxiety"; *Befindlichkeit*: "disposedness"; *Erschlossenheit*: "disclosedness";
  *Entdecktheit*: "uncoveredness"; *Existenz* / *existenzial*: "existence" / "existential"; *kategorial*: "categorial".
- *ontologische Differenz*: "ontological difference"; *ontologischer Unterschied*: "ontological distinction" when SZ's sense (kinds of being) is
  meant and R's translation as *différence* is at issue, otherwise "ontological difference"; *unterscheiden*: "to distinguish".

  *Gegenständlichkeit* (R *objectité*): "objectness"; *Freilegung*: "freeing" (SZ §5 title; GA 20 "Freilegung des Bodens").

- *Destruktion*: "destruction"; *Wiederholung*: "repetition"; *Möglichkeit* / *Wirklichkeit*: "possibility" / "actuality" (Martineau
  *effectivité*, R *effectivité*).
- R *re(con)duction*: kept as R's coinage, glossed "reduction as leading back".
