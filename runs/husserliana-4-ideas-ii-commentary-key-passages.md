## Backfill conventions

- **Unit.** No segmentation list survives from the original run. A unit is the block's ordinal under its lowest heading: "§18 f.2" is the second paragraph under the heading "f)" of §18, "§49 e.4" the fourth under §49 e). Section III's one-line paragraphs ("A one-line paragraph opens the investigation proper") count as blocks.
- **Found by.** Each reason opens with `commentary:`, `source:` or `both:`.
- **Citation.** § and Hua IV page, then the source line (`:n`) of the passage's key sentence. Sections I–II of the commentary cite no pages, so every page there comes from the running heads in the source file. Section III of the commentary cites Hua IV pages itself, and they agree with the file wherever the file has the page.
- **Line pointers** are to the file as restored on 2026-10-28 (`bca34fa`). Pointers up to :3296 are unchanged from the backfill. Later ones moved by +396 when the missing pages were restored, and all of them have been updated.

- **Emphasis is uneven.** The file renders Husserl's spaced type as italics (a few spaced words survive: "o b - *jektiven*", "z u m" at :3741–:3743; "I c h *kann*" at :3871). Italics are dense in §§15, 18, 32, 49 and sparse in §§35–47 and §§57–62 (one to four per section). The print may simply have little emphasis there, but the conversion may also have lost it: in those sections the absence of emphasis is no evidence. - **Headings dropped or altered.** §16 and §29 have no heading in the body: they are on the contents page only (:117, :165). §29's text begins at about :2195, right after §28's heading. The contents page also has *personalistischer* (:175). See row 20.
- **Footnotes.** Notes are collected at page or section ends, and the numbering repeats (three notes all labelled `[^31]` at :3771–:3773). One later annotation, the "Lebenswelt" note of row 49, appears after the §63 heading (:4461) although the commentary attaches it to the end of §62. Locate notes by content, not by marker.
- **More seams and stacked heads in Section I** (found in the close-reading run, 2026-10-28). ## Corrections to the commentary

Added in the close-reading run (2026-10-28):

- §6.2: the block renders "Das Schön sagt nichts weniger als ein *Reflexionsprädikat*" as "'Beautiful' says nothing less than a reflection-predicate", which reverses the sense. *Nichts weniger als* means "anything but": beauty is anything but a reflection-predicate (:712, Hua IV 15). The next sentence of the block ("not relation-predicates") is right.
- §11.6: the block says the scientist's valuing and willing acts "co-determine the full intentional situation". The source says they "belong entirely to the sphere of things" (*gehören … durchaus der Sachsphäre zu*), taken along as objects, and closes with "eine Art Ausschaltung, eine Art ἐποχή" [a kind of exclusion, a kind of epoché], which the block omits (:887, Hua IV 27).
- §17.2: the block omits the clause "nicht substantielle Eigenschaften sind, vielmehr durchaus kausale" [are not substantial properties but rather thoroughly causal ones] (:1323, Hua IV 54). The clause contradicts its context and may be corrupt in the file. The block should flag it rather than pass over it.
- §49 d.1: the block has "the natural — or, as Husserl now says more precisely, the naturalistic — attitude". The source has *naturalen*, not *naturalistischen*: "der natürlichen oder, wie wir jetzt deutlicher sagen, der naturalen Einstellung ebenbürtig" [of equal birth with the natural or, as we now say more distinctly, the nature-directed attitude] (:3224, Hua IV 180). The block also renders *ebenbürtig* [of equal birth] as "of equal rank", which blurs its contrast with §49 e's "not … two equally entitled (*gleichberechtigte*) attitudes" (:3248).
