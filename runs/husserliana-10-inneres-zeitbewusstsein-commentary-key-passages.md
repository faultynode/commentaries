## Scope: which file holds what

- The commentary covers **Part One (Erster Teil) only**: the Introduction and §§1–45. It has no blocks on the Beilagen (supplements) or on Part B. It mentions Beilagen only in passing (§1.6, §16.1, §17.1, §39.3, §40.1, §42.3).
- The Hua X file holds Part One (src L7–L1738), Beilagen I–XIII (src L1739–L2267), and Part B, *Ergänzende Texte* (supplementary texts, from src L2268), followed by the subject index (src L6289). The supplements are therefore in this file, not in the split-off `husserl-texte-zur-phanomenologie-des-inneren-zeitbewusstseins.md`.
- `husserl-texte-zur-phanomenologie-des-inneren-zeitbewusstseins.md` (Bernet ed., Meiner PhB 362) holds Bernet's introduction and **Hua X's editorial apparatus**: *Zur Textgestaltung* (on the constitution of the text, its L695), the table of manuscript sources per § (its L757ff.), and the *Textkritische Anmerkungen* (text-critical notes) keyed to Hua X page and line (its L804ff.). Criterion (e) rows cite it as "Texte L n".

## Backfill conventions

- **Unit.** No segmentation list survives from the original run. "§6.7" is the seventh block under the §6 heading. Every non-empty paragraph counts as a block; bullet items and `---` rules do not. The Introduction's own blocks are "Intro.1–3".
- **Found by.** Each reason starts with `commentary:`, `source:` or `both:`.
- **Citation.** § and Hua X page, then `src L n` = the line of the passage's key sentence in the source file, read for this list.
- **Translations** are my own working renderings. Originals are kept only where the nomination turns on them.

## Source caveats for Stage 2 (and for any close reading)

- **Pages are ±1.** There are no page breaks. Pages come from running-head comments (`<!-- 4 ZUR PHÄNOMENOLOGIE … -->`, `<!-- DIE VORLESUNGEN AUS DEM JAHRE 1905 | 7 -->`), which sometimes sit mid-line or at the foot of the page they close. Many paragraphs are a single long line spanning a page break. Bracketed numbers such as `[378]` are the 1928 *Jahrbuch* first-edition pages, not Hua X pages.
- **Emphasis is lost.** Hua X sets emphasis in spaced type. The file has no spaced type at all, and only 22 italic-bearing lines in Part One, almost all of them titles, Latin (*in infinitum*, *quasi*, *mutatis mutandis*, *eo ipso*) or Augustine's name. The apparatus records restored emphasis the file does not show (e.g. Texte L1393: "75,9, 10, 15f., 17 u. 18 Hervorhebungen nach dem Ms." [emphases after the manuscript] in §36). **No row argues from emphasis, and the absence of emphasis in the file proves nothing.**
- **Two conversion grains.** §§1–33 are mostly one line per paragraph. From §34 onward the text is hard-wrapped at print width, with end-of-line hyphenation (e.g. "Quer-/intentionalität", src L1353–1354). Grep for a hyphenated coinage across both forms.
- **§31: text missing at src L993.** The paragraph breaks off at "das individualisierende Moment der ursprünglichen." [the individuating moment of the original.], and the next line is the running head for p. 69, then §31's last paragraph. About a page of Hua X (pp. 67, l. 35 to 68) is absent: the apparatus (Texte L1357) annotates lines 68,3–68,36 that the file does not contain. The commentary's §31.7 paraphrases the missing part (the original impression of temporal position; past-modification compared to phantasy-modification; the identity of the individual as identity of temporal position). Row 18 is affected.
- **Suspected OCR error at src L462 (§12).** "die relationalen ,,Inhalte''" [the relational "contents"] where the argument and the commentary (§12.2) read "retentional". Not repaired here; check the print before quoting.
- **Orthography seam at src L1427 (§40).** That line spells "Bewusstseinsinhalte … Urbewusstseins" with *ss*, where the rest of the file has *ß*. It may be a hand-patched line. Check before quoting.
- **Beilage VI has no heading.** Its text (pp. 111 ff.) runs on under the Beilage V heading (src L1880–L1951). Not used by any row.
- **Formulas.** The §18 notation is partly LaTeX (`$(A—B)'$`, src L584; `$$[(A - B) - (A - B)']'.$$`, src L604).
- **Stage 2 searched**: italic markup; ,,…'' and „…“ quotation marks; hyphenated coinages (*Vor-Zugleich*, *Ur-gezeugt-Entsprungenes*, *Soeben-gewesen*); Latin set off in the text; the phrasing *heißt* [is called, means], *besagt* [says, means], *bedeutet* [means], *das Wort* [the word], *Name(n)* [name(s)], *im Bilde* [in the image]; and the names Brentano, Augustine, Herbart, Lotze, Stern, Marty, Stumpf, Mach, Meinong, Kant, Hume, James. There is no Greek in Part One. Spaced type could not be searched, because the file has none.

## Stage 1 note

The commentary keeps almost no original wording: four parenthesized German words in 383 lines ("reell" §6.7, "Vor-Zugleich" §38.2, "Urbewusstsein" §40.1, and "phantasm" in English at §12.3). Criterion (d) therefore comes almost entirely from Stage 2. Lexicon terms (311 Husserl entries in `synthesis/lexicon.json`) were grepped. Most first hits are incidental uses in the Introduction and §1. The rows below take the first block that discusses a term (retention §8.1, primal impression §11.1, primal consciousness §16.1/§40.1, longitudinal and transverse intentionality §39, living present §25.2), not the first that uses it.

## Considered and dropped

These qualify but were dropped as weaker than the rows above, per the "strongest few" rule.

- Intro.1: Augustine, with the Latin dictum kept in the source (*si nemo a me quaerat, scio* [if no one asks me, I know], src L13). It is (c), but nothing later in the lectures depends on it, and the commentary's "distension of the soul" is its own addition.
- §1.8: the chalk example. It is (b), but row 2 carries the content–apprehension point.
- §2.2: "origin" as epistemic source, not causal beginning, and the a priori laws later derived in §33. It is (a), but the term's work is done in §6 and §33.
- §5.1: Brentano's determining and modifying predicates ("a past red is not a red"). It is (c), but it is folded into row 5.
