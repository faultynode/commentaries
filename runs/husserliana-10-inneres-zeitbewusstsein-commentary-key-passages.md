## Source caveats for Stage 2 (and for any close reading)

- **Pages are ±1.** There are no page breaks. Pages come from running-head comments (`<!-- 4 ZUR PHÄNOMENOLOGIE … -->`, `<!-- DIE VORLESUNGEN AUS DEM JAHRE 1905 | 7 -->`; "on the phenomenology of internal time-consciousness", "the lectures of 1905"), which sometimes sit mid-line or at the foot of the page they close. Many paragraphs are a single long line spanning a page break. Bracketed numbers such as `[378]` are the 1928 *Jahrbuch* first-edition pages, not Hua X pages.
- **Emphasis is lost.** Hua X sets emphasis in spaced type. The file has no spaced type at all, and only 22 italic-bearing lines in Part One, almost all of them titles, Latin (*in infinitum* [to infinity], *quasi* [as it were], *mutatis mutandis* [with the necessary changes], *eo ipso* [by that very fact]) or Augustine's name. The apparatus records restored emphasis the file does not show (e.g. Texte L1393: "75,9, 10, 15f., 17 u. 18 Hervorhebungen nach dem Ms." [emphases after the manuscript] in §36). **No row argues from emphasis, and the absence of emphasis in the file proves nothing.**
- **Two conversion grains.** §§1–33 are mostly one line per paragraph. From §34 onward the text is hard-wrapped at print width, with end-of-line hyphenation (e.g. "Quer-/intentionalität" [transverse intentionality], src L1353–1354). Grep for a hyphenated coinage across both forms.
- **§31: text missing at src L993.** The paragraph breaks off at "das individualisierende Moment der ursprünglichen." [the individuating moment of the original.], and the next line is the running head for p. 69, then §31's last paragraph. About a page of Hua X (pp. 67, l. 35 to 68) is absent: the apparatus (Texte L1357) annotates lines 68,3–68,36 that the file does not contain. The commentary's §31.7 paraphrases the missing part (the original impression of temporal position; past-modification compared to phantasy-modification; the identity of the individual as identity of temporal position). Row 16 is affected. Before logging this as an absence, the whole file and the Texte file were grepped for fragments of the missing text ("Zeitstellenimpression" [temporal-position impression], "Identität der Zeitstelle" [identity of the temporal position], "Phantasiemodifikation" [phantasy-modification], "nichts für sich" [nothing by itself], "Hervorquellen neuer" [welling up of new]). No copy turned up elsewhere. Only the apparatus (Texte L1357) and §31.1 (src L937) match. CW4 has the passage in English (CW4 L9305ff.: "the impression of the original temporal position … is naturally nothing by itself").
- **Suspected OCR error at src L462 (§12).** "die relationalen ,,Inhalte''" [the relational "contents"] where the argument and the commentary (§12.2) read "retentional". Not repaired here; check the print before quoting.
- **Orthography seam at src L1427 (§40).** That line spells "Bewusstseinsinhalte … Urbewusstseins" [contents of consciousness … of primal consciousness] with *ss*, where the rest of the file has *ß*. It may be a hand-patched line. Check before quoting.

- **Formulas.** The §18 notation is partly LaTeX (`$(A—B)'$`, src L584; `$$[(A - B) - (A - B)']'.$$`, src L604).

- Intro.1: Augustine, with the Latin dictum kept in the source (*si nemo a me quaerat, scio* [if no one asks me, I know], src L13). It is (c), but nothing later in the lectures depends on it, and the commentary's "distension of the soul" is its own addition.
- §1.8: the chalk example. It is (b), but row 2 carries the content–apprehension point.
- §2.2: "origin" as epistemic source, not causal beginning, and the a priori laws later derived in §33. It is (a), but the term's work is done in §6 and §33.

- §16.1: "adequate perception" of the melody, and the first "primal consciousness" (*Urbewußtsein*, src L532). It is (a, b), but row 12 states the result, and the term's sense is fixed at §40 (row 24).
- §19.3: the two axes, the continuous now-to-past modification and the discrete impression-to-reproduction one (src L651). It is (b), but it restates row 12 in structural form.
- §23.3: the second intentionality peculiar to presentifications. It is (a), but it is subordinate to rows 9 and 22.
- §28.1: memory as "self-presentification" (*Selbstvergegenwärtigung*, src L883–L886). It is (a), but it is a confirmation, not a step.
- §33.2: "the creative time-point, the source-point of temporal positions in general" (src L1054). It is (d), but it repeats row 10's metaphor.

## Corrections to the commentary

- §38.2: the block renders Husserl's distinction as "the fluxional pre-simultaneity from the impressional simultaneity of fluxions" and adds "Neither may be called simultaneity proper". The source's second term is a *Zugleich* (together, at once), not a simultaneity: "zwischen fluxionalem Vor-Zugleich und impressionalem Zugleich von Fluxionen scheiden. Wir können nicht ein oder das andere Zugleich ein Gleichzeitig nennen" [distinguish between the fluxional pre-together and the impressional together of fluxions. We cannot call either the one or the other together a simultaneous]. So the rendering gives "simultaneity" the very word Husserl withholds, and "simultaneity proper" softens an outright refusal into a matter of degree. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-10-vorlesungen-zur-phanomenologie-des-inneren-zeitbewusstseins.md:1242–1245.
- §31.8: the block says that the phrase "living present", "central in the late manuscripts, has its root here" (§31). §31 does not use the phrase: it has "in the living source-point of being, the now" (*in dem lebendigen Quellpunkt des Seins, dem Jetzt*, :997). "Living present" (*lebendige Gegenwart*) first occurs at §25, in the whole stream "up to the living present" (:819), which the commentary itself paraphrases at §25.2. Source: sources/husserl/husserl-hua-10-vorlesungen-zur-phanomenologie-des-inneren-zeitbewusstseins.md:819, :997.
