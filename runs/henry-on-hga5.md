### Misreading guards (Henry, *L'essence de la manifestation*, on GA 5)

1. **Three voices in P.** The essay is Heidegger commenting on Hegel's Introduction paragraph by paragraph. A sentence Henry quotes as "dit Heidegger" may be Hegel's own text (printed in the essay), Heidegger's exposition of Hegel in Hegel's terms (consciousness, appearing, natural and real knowledge, the absolute), or Heidegger's being-historical frame (Parousia read as presence, subjectity as the epoch of modern metaphysics, the ontological difference). Fix which before judging Henry's use.
2. **Exposition is not doctrine.** Henry's Section I takes the essay's exposition of modern metaphysics as Heidegger's own eidetic of consciousness: "Das Vor-stellen waltet in allen Weisen des Bewußtseins" (representing governs in all modes of consciousness, H 133 / GA 5 145) and the subject's being as presence in the mode of representation (H 134 / GA 5 146) become, in R §11, monism's definition of the essence. In the essay these state what "subject" means for Descartes-to-Hegel. Henry's move may still hold (Heidegger's own thinking of appearing may stay within the frame he expounds), but it is Henry's thesis and must be argued at each locus, not read off the quotation.
3. **Subjectity is not subjectivity.** Heidegger coins *Subjektität* for the being of the subject as reflected subject-object relation, "die Präsenz in der Weise der Repräsentation" (presence in the mode of representation, H 134 / GA 5 146). Henry writes "subjectivité" ("La subjectivité du sujet, dit Heidegger, c'est l'*Erscheinen* lui-même", the subjectivity of the subject is appearing itself, R §11, n. 66). Check the wording at H 134 each time; the rendering erases the coinage that marks the term as the epoch's.
4. **Ontic and ontological are the essay's own mapping, and Henry uses it twice over.** H 161-163 call natural consciousness "ontisches Bewußtsein" (ontic consciousness) and its unthought representing of objectness ontological. In Section I (R §12, nn. 82-83) Henry quotes this approvingly, with his own emphasis, as the omission of being in naive knowing; in Section II (R §17) he contests the same pages by substituting existential for ontic. Track which use a unit makes, and do not let the Section II verdict leak back into Section I.
5. **The reversal belongs to Hegel's text before it is Heidegger's.** *Umkehrung des Bewußtseins* (reversal of consciousness) is Hegel's phrase in the Introduction; Heidegger reads it as consciousness turning toward the appearing of what appears (H 189-190 / GA 5 205-206). R §13 n. 95 quotes the gloss as Heidegger's position on Dasein. Mark whose reversal a unit discusses.
6. **Chronology.** The essay comes from 1942-43 seminars and was first published in *Holzwege* (1950); Henry used that edition. He could not know the GA 5 marginalia (1977), the 1930-31 Hegel lecture course GA 32 (1980), or GA 68 (1993; it holds "Die Negativität", 1938-39, and the 1942 "Erläuterung der ›Einleitung‹ zu Hegels ›Phänomenologie des Geistes‹"). GA 32 and GA 68 are in the corpus in English (GA 68 also in French, the `-b` file); any use of them is retrospective and marked so.
7. **Henry joins this essay to the Kantbuch.** EM reads the 1942-43 Hegel essay and the 1929 Kantbuch as one "philosophy of transcendence"; Section I §13 sets H next to SZ, Section II §28 next to K. Note where the turn between them matters, especially on presence, history (R §21's "non-historical character" is aimed at the essay's history of the absolute's Parousia) and forgetting.
8. **Henry's French is his own.** Every H rendering in EM is Henry's, so a tilt in it counts as his move unless the 1962 French translation is shown to carry it. Settled in the GA 3 run, to re-verify: *Darstellung* as "représentation" (H 174-181), *Sicherscheinen des Erscheinens* as "s'apparaître de l'apparaître" (H 170, 173), "Schauplatz" turned into "le champ où l'Erscheinen parvient à l'intuition de soi" and "gebildet" into "fait" (H 134), and the cuts at H 126 ("schon"), H 163 ("doch noch", "vorontologisch"), H 167 ("schon zu sein"), H 189-190.

### Glossary (fixed for this run)

- Translation rule: every German or French quotation carries an English rendering, in the citation parenthesis ("German" (English, H 126 / GA 5 137)) or as an apposition. Single quoted words count. A quotation repeated within one paragraph may rely on its first rendering there. In the output (a commentary file) English leads, per prompts/commentary-prompt.md; in this run file and in chat the original leads.
- Henry *étant* and Heidegger *Seiendes*: "beings", "a being". Henry *étantité* (R §11, for H 161) and Heidegger *Seiendheit*: "beingness".
- Henry *l'essence*: "the essence" (of manifestation), always Henry's term. Henry *monisme ontologique*: "ontological monism". *Distance phénoménologique*: "phenomenological distance".
- Heidegger *Erscheinen*: "appearing" (Henry keeps "l'Erscheinen"). Hegel *Erscheinung*: "appearance"; Hyppolite's "manifestation" is marked as his. *Sicherscheinen*: "self-appearing". *Schein*: "semblance" (Henry "apparence").
- *Parusie*: "Parousia". *Anwesen*, *Anwesenheit*: "presencing", "presence". *Präsenz*: "presence" (marked where it stands for Heidegger's Latinate word). *Repräsentation*: "representation" (marked).
- *Vorstellen*, *Vor-stellen*: "representing" (hyphen kept when Heidegger hyphenates). *Darstellung*: "presentation" (Henry "représentation", marked each time).
- *Subjektität*: "subjectity" (Henry "subjectivité", marked). *Subjektivität*: "subjectivity".
- *natürliches Bewußtsein*: "natural consciousness"; *reales Wissen*: "real knowledge"; *erscheinendes Wissen*: "appearing knowledge"; *ontisches* / *ontologisches Bewußtsein*: "ontic" / "ontological consciousness"; *vorontologisch*: "pre-ontological".
- *Umkehrung*: "reversal" (Henry "renversement"). *Zutat*: "addition". *Zusehen*: "looking-on". *Stätte*: "site". *Schauplatz*: "stage". *Wesensspielraum*: "essential play-space". *Entäußerung*: "externalization" in Hegel's German, "alienation" when reporting Hyppolite's and Henry's "aliénation".
- Retained untranslated: Dasein (being-there).
