## Backfill conventions

- **Unit.** No segmentation list survives from the original run. A unit is the block's ordinal under its lowest heading: "§9h.3" is the third non-empty paragraph under the "#### h)" heading of § 9; "§34.5" is the fifth paragraph under § 34 (the commentary's lettered sub-points there are paragraphs, not headings). Bullet items and `---` rules do not count.
- **Citation.** The commentary cites only by § (and § 9's lettered subsections); it gives no Hua pages. The citation column gives the §, then `src L<n>`, the source line of the passage's key sentence, read in this run. "Hua VI n" is the page of the nearest running head before that line (`<!-- … n -->`), so ±1: the file joins page lines into long paragraph lines, so a head can fall after the start of the paragraph it heads. Confirm against print before citing a page.
- **Found by.** Each reason opens with `commentary:`, `source:` or `both:`.
- **Status.** Every row is `nominated`. Only the user approves or rejects.
- **Translations** of German in this file are my working renderings, not a published translation. English leads, the original follows in parentheses, and the original is kept only where the nomination turns on it.

## Corrections to the commentary

- §9i.1–2: the blocks say §9i continues the "discovering and concealing genius" theme and Galileo's inheritance of an already technized geometry (that is §9h material, src L1037). The source's §9i is about Galileo's doctrine of the mere subjectivity of the specific sense-qualities, which Hobbes extended to all concrete phenomena of intuited nature, and then about the physicists' self-interpretation of nature as mathematical "in itself", with its "innate" and non-innate mathematics. Source: husserl-hua-6-…phanomenologie.md:1048–1060. **Fixed 2026-11-04** (commit 2cf3902): §9i rewritten from the source, now five blocks (old §9i.1–2 → §9i.1–5) on the subjectivity of sense-qualities (Galileo, Hobbes) and the physicists' self-interpretation; no row cites §9i.
- §9l.1: the block says Husserl calls his procedure an "intentional history" (*intentionale Historie*). The phrase does not occur in §9l, nor anywhere in the Hua VI file (whole-file grep for "intentionale Historie", "intentional-histor", "Historie"). §9l describes the method as going forward and back "in a zigzag" (*im „Zickzack“*). The nearest wording is in Beilage III, "historical in an unaccustomed sense" (*historisch in einem ungewohnten Sinn*, :4159). Source: husserl-hua-6-…phanomenologie.md:1074–1076. **Fixed 2026-11-04** (commit 2cf3902): "intentional history" removed; the block now has Husserl going forward and back "in a zigzag".
- §28.3: the block puts in §28 the description of the lived body "designated by the word 'organ' in its original meaning: that wherein I… immediately kinaesthetically govern". That is §62, not §28: "only it is originally given to me with sense as 'organ', and as articulated into part-organs" (*Nur er ist ursprünglich sinnhaft mir gegeben als ,,Organ'', und als gegliedert in Teilorgane*, with *walten* [govern] just before). §28 mentions only the body with its "perceptual organs" (*Wahrnehmungsorganen*, :1666). Source: husserl-hua-6-…phanomenologie.md:3002 (misattribution).
- §54.3: the block resolves the paradox by saying that the constituting ego "is the human ego itself seen from within the transcendental attitude", one subjectivity seen in two attitudes. The source says the ego reached in the epoché "is called 'I' only by equivocation". It is "not *an* I" that has its You and We, but the primal I (*Ur-Ich*), unique and "personally undeclinable". The error of the first approach was "jumping straight into transcendental intersubjectivity and skipping the primal I". The human being is that ego's self-objectivation, not the same ego in another attitude. Source: husserl-hua-6-…phanomenologie.md:2652–2684.
- §55 (heading, §55.1–2): the heading reads "…through the way over the 'life-world'", and the blocks take the correction to be that the life-world is itself a constitutive formation. The source's title is "The principled correction of our first approach to the epoché by reducing it to the absolutely unique, ultimately functioning ego" (*durch Reduktion derselben auf das absolut einzige letztlich fungierende ego*). The text calls for "a second" epoché, or a conscious reshaping of the first, "by reduction to the absolute ego as the ultimately unique functional centre of all constitution". Source: husserl-hua-6-…phanomenologie.md:2716–2722.
- §42 (heading): the commentary's title, "The task of the concrete preliminary sketch of the dimensions of the working problems by means of the discovery of the correlation", is not the source's. The source has "The task of concretely sketching in advance ways of actually carrying out the transcendental reduction" (*Die Aufgabe der konkreten Vorzeichnung von Wegen einer wirklichen Durchführung der transzendentalen Reduktion*). Source: husserl-hua-6-…phanomenologie.md:2122.
- §62 (heading, §62.1): the commentary's title is "Preliminary discussion of the countersense of the principled naturalization of consciousness", and the block discusses psychophysical parallelism, epiphenomenalism and "functionalism". The source's title is about "the countersense of the principled equal ranking of souls and bodies as realities", with a "reference to the principled difference of temporality, causality, individuation in natural thing and soul" (*Widersinn der prinzipiellen Gleichstellung von Seelen und Körpern als Realitäten*). Its text (e.g. :3002 on spatio-temporality) works through those three differences. Source: husserl-hua-6-…phanomenologie.md:2996.
