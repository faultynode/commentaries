## Backfill conventions

- **Unit.** No segmentation list survives from the original run. A unit is the block's ordinal under its lowest heading: "§9h.3" is the third non-empty paragraph under the "#### h)" heading of § 9; "§34.5" is the fifth paragraph under § 34 (the commentary's lettered sub-points there are paragraphs, not headings). Bullet items and `---` rules do not count.
- **Citation.** The commentary cites only by § (and § 9's lettered subsections); it gives no Hua pages. The citation column gives the §, then `src L<n>`, the source line of the passage's key sentence, read in this run. "Hua VI n" is the page of the nearest running head before that line (`<!-- … n -->`), so ±1: the file joins page lines into long paragraph lines, so a head can fall after the start of the paragraph it heads. Confirm against print before citing a page.
- **Found by.** Each reason opens with `commentary:`, `source:` or `both:`.
- **Status.** Every row is `nominated`. Only the user approves or rejects.
- **Translations** of German in this file are my working renderings, not a published translation. English leads, the original follows in parentheses, and the original is kept only where the nomination turns on it.

## Source caveats for Stage 2 (and for any close reading)

- **Lost pages, with seams spliced mid-sentence.** These are not listed in `sources/CLAUDE.md` or `conversion-history.md`. Each was confirmed by grepping the whole file for distinctive fragments of the missing text:
  - **Hua VI 15 (§7 opening).** L595 ends §6 ("…bis in unsere Gegenwart forterstreckt"), and the next page (L597, head "16") begins mid-sentence: "glauben zu können, wir, die wir glauben". The § 7 heading, and the text behind commentary §7.1–7.3 ("functionaries of humanity"), are not in the file. The only "Funktionäre" in the file is §15's different phrase, "functionaries of modern philosophical humanity" (*Funktionäre der neuzeitlichen philosophischen Menschheit*, L1284).
  - **Hua VI ~46–47 (§9f–g).** L898 runs "…in aller ‚konkret sachhaltigen' reinen Mathematik, der setze hin. Aber auch die Experimentalphysiker…". The card-and-chess-game comparison in commentary §9g.1 is nowhere in the file.
  - **§29 (Hua VI ~114–115).** At L1712, "Erfüllt die Philosophie ihren Urstiftungssinn als universal und sentlich zu dieser weltkonstituierenden Leistung…" joins two pages. The "anonymity" question of commentary §29.2 is lost, and "anonym" survives only in the heading (L1706).
  - **§36 (Hua VI ~143).** At L2007, "Das Katoriale der Lebenswelt allen objektiven Wissenschaften, wenn wir sie auch…" joins two pages. The "same names" sentence in commentary §36.2 is not in the file.
  - **§72–73 (Hua VI ~269–274).** At L3336, "Die Phänomenologie befreit vom alten objektivistischen Ideal des zu erheben berufen ist…" joins two pages. §73's heading and opening, the I/You/We passage, the stages of self-responsibility, and the "dawn" and "tautology" paragraphs (commentary §73.1–2, §73.5–6) are absent. Only L3337–3347 (Hua VI 271/275–276: "the in-itself", psychology's false objectivity, idealism) survive. The pronoun passage has an inverted parallel in §54 (L2652–2655: the epoché-ego is *not* an I that still has its You and We), which is not the same text.
  
  Other page gaps are likely: running heads are missing for about 60 pages of the main text. Not every missing head is a lost page, and no full audit was made. Check continuity at any passage before quoting it.
- **Lost § headings.** Grepping the titles from the table of contents (L57–L292) finds no heading line in the body for §§7, 9, 17, 18, 24, 31, 37, 45, 48, 53, 54, 57, 59, 60, 72 and 73. §67's title is merged into a paragraph (L3103), and several others (§§1, 8, 12, 14, 28, 36, 46, 55, 56, 68–71) are plain or bold text, not `#` headings. Grepping `^#` does not map the §s.
- **Emphasis.** Italics survive (about 249 italic-bearing lines in L375–3347). Spaced type is rendered three ways: as bold with the word spaces collapsed (L672 "**Reichereiner Limesgestalten**", L1378), as letter-spacing (L2652 "e i n", L3117 "rein e n"), or merged into italic (L570–571 "E n-*telechie*"). Emphasis can be argued from, but which kind (italic or spaced) cannot be relied on.
- **Layout.** Pages alternate between long joined paragraph lines and print lines with end-of-line hyphenation. Some carry print line numbers ("5", "10" … "35") at the start (e.g. L1735, L2650, L3322–3336). Grep with hyphenation variants. Running-head page numbers are ±1 (see Citation).
- **OCR damage seen (not repaired):** L507 "als Ermöglichkeit des Menschen, seinem… Dasein vernünftig zu verschaffen" (a word apparently lost); L1284 "teelogische"; L1735 "Subjektiyen"; L2007 "Katoriale"; L3337 "An-such". The Greek is clean Unicode where seen (L503 ὄντως ὄν, L1064 τέχνη).
- **Footnotes** are `[^n]` with numbers repeated per page, so a footnote number does not resolve on its own. The 1898 note (row 20) is at L2378–2384.
- **Stage 2 searched:** italic and bold markup, letter-spaced words, „…" and ,,…" quotation marks, hyphenated coinages (*Ur-*, *Ent-*), Greek, Latin set off in the text (*more geometrico*, *ego cogito*, *animal rationale*), the phrasing *heißt* [means], *besagt* [says, means], *das Wort* [the word], *ursprünglichen Sinn* [original sense], and the names Galilei, Descartes, Locke, Berkeley, Hume, Kant, Leibniz, Helmholtz, Brentano and James. **It could not search** the lost pages, nor distinguish spaced type from italic where the two were merged.

## Considered and dropped

- §7.3: "functionaries of humanity" (*Funktionäre der Menschheit*). It is (b, d), and the strongest block in §7, but the source file has lost the page that carries it (Hua VI 15; see caveats). A close reading would be blocked.
- §7.5: breaking through the crust of the "historical facts" to their hidden teleology (*verborgene Teleologie*, src L597). It is (b), but the method is stated more fully in §15 (Part II).
- §6.4: "lazy reason" (*faule Vernunft*, italic, src L586), turned against irrationalism. It is (c), but the block has no later dependants.
