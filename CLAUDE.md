## Original-language wording (standing rule)

Applies to every commentary, in `commentaries/` and `outputs/` alike, and
to every commentary skill and prompt.

- Write in English. Every non-English quotation is translated.
- Keep the original only for terms of art in the glossary, and for
  wording whose sense departs from the English in a way the argument
  turns on (a contested rendering, a pun or etymology, a phrase turned
  against another author, a difference between editions).
- **Test: delete the original.** If a reader with no French or German
  loses nothing, leave it out and quote or paraphrase in English.
- Where kept, the original follows the English in parentheses.

Full statement and examples: [commentaries/CLAUDE.md](commentaries/CLAUDE.md)
§"When to keep the original".
