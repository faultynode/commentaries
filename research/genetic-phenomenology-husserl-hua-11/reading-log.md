### Read in full

- `:655`–`:680`, end of § 4 (pp. 23–24). The close of the Introduction names
  two tasks. Constitutive analyses make understandable how perception brings
  its sense-giving about; genetic analyses make understandable how "in der zum
  Wesen jedes Bewußtseinsstromes gehörigen Entwicklung, die zugleich
  Ichentwicklung ist" [in the development that belongs to the essence of every
  stream of consciousness, which is at once a development of the ego] the
  intentional systems develop through which an outer world can appear
  (`:672`). *Surprise:* the genetic task is announced "nur mit einem Wort"
  [only in a word] and for transcendent reality, which the lectures then do
  not carry out (§ 48 says so, `:2979`).
- `:1200`–`:1335`, §§ 16–18 (pp. 65–77; lecture after Christmas 1925, n. at
  `:1266`). Evidence in passivity. `:1283`: the analyses lead to "universalsten
  Strukturgesetzmäßigkeiten" [most universal structural laws] of transcendental
  inner life "aber auch zu den universalsten Gesetzmäßigkeiten der Genesis"
  [but also to the most universal laws of genesis]: structure and genesis named
  side by side. `:1292`: one may not set perception, recollection,
  co-presentation and expectation side by side as types; they must be
  understood "als Strukturzusammenhang" [as a structural nexus]. `:1310`:
  retention's necessary attachment to intuition is "ein Grundgesetz der
  passiven Genesis" [a basic law of passive genesis]. `:1314`: "Urgenesis des
  Zeitstromes" [primal genesis of the time-stream]; protention is "die zweite
  Seite der genetischen Urgesetzlichkeit" [the second side of the primal
  genetic lawfulness]. `:1316`: intuitive memories do not come first in
  genesis; "das wesensmäßige Früher" [the essentially earlier] are the empty
  presentations. `:1321`: "Genetisch sagen wir auch mit Grund, die Wahrnehmung
  habe die Vorstellung geweckt" [genetically we also say, with reason, that the
  perception has awakened the presentation], immediately restated as the
  arising of a "Richtungssynthese" [synthesis of direction]. `:1328`: the
  "passive Intention" and association as "völlig universal fungierende Gestalt
  passiver Genesis" [a wholly universally functioning form of passive
  genesis]. *Surprise:* the genetic "earlier" is called essential
  (wesensmäßig), not temporal-factual, and the genetic statement and the
  structural statement are given for the same state of affairs in one
  sentence.
- `:1886`–`:2003`, end of § 25, §§ 26–27 (pp. 114–128). `:1893`: the origin of
  error in passivity (mixing of memories) leads to "ein radikales Stück der
  passiven Bewußtseinsanalyse, und zwar als genetischer Analyse" [a radical
  piece of the analysis of passive consciousness, namely as genetic analysis]:
  the phenomenology of association. So association enters as the answer to a
  question of *truth* (the in-itself of the past). `:1911`–`:1913`: association
  is "Form und Gesetzmäßigkeit der immanenten Genesis" [a form and lawfulness
  of immanent genesis], not psychophysical causality. `:1932`: access to "einer
  universalen Theorie der Genesis einer reinen Subjektivität" [a universal
  theory of the genesis of a pure subjectivity], first in its lower layer of
  pure passivity; the phenomenology of association is "eine höhere Fortführung
  der Lehre von der ursprünglichen Zeitkonstitution" [a higher continuation of
  the doctrine of original time-constitution]; Kant saw the transcendental
  necessity of association; Husserl does not claim the genetic problems are
  solved, only that the method of solution can be sketched. `:1934`: the facts
  of association are at first "transzendentale Fakta" [transcendental facts];
  eidetic insight comes after. `:1938`: genesis of reproductions; of
  expectations; of apperceptions; "Genesis der Vorgriffsphänomene" [genesis of
  the phenomena of anticipation]; inductive association. `:1940`: Aristotle's
  first in itself and first for us, "vom Standpunkt der erklärenden (der
  verstehbar machenden) Erkenntnis" [from the standpoint of explanatory
  (making-understandable) knowledge]. `:1944`: "Ferner gibt sich das Phänomen
  selbst eine Genesis" [further, the phenomenon gives itself a genesis]: one
  member as awakening, the other as awakened. `:1966`: the "genetischen
  Bedingungen" [genetic conditions] of the possibility of recollection are the
  conditions of the possibility of a subjectivity that is for itself. `:1978`:
  time-synthesis is the A in "ABC der Konstitution". `:1982`: "die Genesis einer
  Subjektivität (die doch nur in Genesis denkbar ist)" [the genesis of a
  subjectivity (which is thinkable only in genesis)]; Kant's A-deduction.
  `:1997`: the analysis of time-consciousness is "von vornherein eine
  abstrakte" [abstract from the outset]: it abstracts from content.
  *Surprise:* "transcendental" and "genetic" are joined: syntheses are
  transcendental "als apodiktisch notwendig für die Genesis einer
  Subjektivität" [as apodictically necessary for the genesis of a
  subjectivity].
- `:2003`–`:2080`, §§ 28–29 (pp. 128–138). `:2006`: recollection is set aside,
  to be taken up "erst nachher in ihrer Genese" [only afterwards in its
  genesis]; "Zu Gesicht bekommen, direkt erfassen können wir ja nur, wo wir
  für sich Abgehobenes haben" [we can only get to see, grasp directly, where
  we have something prominent for itself]. `:2012`, `:2018`: "statische" against
  "kinetische Betrachtung" [static against kinetic consideration] of
  homogeneity; what is statically there as likeness is "Deckung par distance"
  [coincidence at a distance]. `:2024`: "Überschiebung als Methode der
  Enthüllung" [overlaying as a method of disclosure]. `:2076`: the living
  present "die ja selbst das universalste genetische Phänomen ist" [which is
  itself the most universal genetic phenomenon]. *Surprise:* "static" here is
  not the 1921 sense (correlation analysis) but a contrast inside the lecture's
  own procedure (static/kinetic); the word has two uses in the volume.
- `:2178`–`:2227`, §§ 32–33 (pp. 148–153). `:2202`: "Wir, in unserer
  Betrachtung unterster genetischer Stufe, stellen das Problem in der für eine
  systematische Genesis notwendigen Abstraktion: Wir tun so, als ob die Welt
  des Ich nur die impressionale Gegenwart wäre" [We, in our consideration of
  the lowest genetic level, pose the problem in the abstraction necessary for
  a systematic genesis: we act as if the world of the ego were only the
  impressional present]. `:2214`: form and content conditions are only
  "Bedingungen der Möglichkeit" [conditions of possibility] of hyletic unity;
  actual unit formation would still depend on affection. *Surprise:* the
  "lowest genetic level" is reached by an as-if, not by going back in time.
- `:2299`–`:2395`, §§ 34–35 (pp. 159–171). Two readings of the conditions of
  affection are tried and the second rejected as "unverständliche
  Substruktionen" [unintelligible substructions] that would leave "die
  Wesenssphäre" [the sphere of essence] (`:2331`); "Nach diesem methodischen
  Prinzip" (`:2333`). `:2343`: a radical theory would solve "das Rätsel der
  Assoziation und damit alle Rätsel des 'Unbewußten'" [the riddle of
  association and so all riddles of the "unconscious"]. `:2345`: "alle Motive
  einer Theorie entspringen aus den Evidenzen der ersten Stufe ... den
  Phänomenen der sozusagen fertig strukturierten lebendigen Gegenwart" [all
  motives of a theory spring from the evidences of the first level ... the
  phenomena of the, so to speak, ready-structured living present]. Husserl's
  own later note `:2353` restricts § 34's result to the living present.
  `:2393`: empty retention "die genetische Urform von Leervorstellungen" [the
  genetic primal form of empty presentations]. *Surprise:* the genetic theory
  is tied by a stated rule to what is evident in the finished present.
- `:2926`–`:2990`, § 48 (pp. 218–222). `:2934`: "Der Leitgedanke": nothing
  becomes conscious without consciousness having accomplished "die
  entsprechende intentionale Genesis" [the corresponding intentional genesis],
  whose "Niederschlag" [sediment] is the retentional system "in welchem die
  Vorbedingungen für das Ansich ... und für seine Normierung liegen" [in which
  lie the preconditions for the in-itself ... and for its norming].
  Consciousness "eine nie abbrechende Geschichte" [a never-ending history];
  history is "von einer immanenten Teleologie durchherrschtes Konstituieren"
  [a constituting governed by an immanent teleology]. `:2955`–`:2961`: the work
  "vor Weihnachten" gave "ein sozusagen statisches Verständnis" [a so to speak
  static understanding] of "fertige Intentionalität" [finished
  intentionality]. `:2979`: the problem of genesis "wurde uns ... empfindlich"
  [became sensible to us] in the self-objectivation of consciousness as to its
  past; the same "müßte nun auch für die Konstitution der Natur geleistet
  werden" [would now have to be done for the constitution of nature too].
  `:2981`: all the transcendental disciplines treat their problems "statisch
  und genetisch". *Surprise:* the lectures' own retrospect says the genetic
  work was done only for immanent time and the past, and done under the
  question of the in-itself.
- `:4427`–`:4523`, "Statische und genetische phänomenologische Methode" (pp.
  336–345; dated 1921, n. `:4517`). Laws of genesis in two senses (succession;
  formation of apperceptions). "Geschichte des Bewußtseins" is not factual
  genesis but essential: "nur gegeben der Modus der Genesis" [only the mode of
  genesis is given] in which an apperception of a type must have originally
  arisen (`:4455`). "erklärende" against "beschreibende"; "In den Vorlesungen
  sagte ich nicht 'beschreibende', sondern 'statische' Phänomenologie"
  (`:4463`). No shape of active apperception can be described "ohne daß dabei
  beständig von Genesis die Rede ist" (`:4470`). Programme in seven points
  (`:4477`–`:4494`). "Urgesetze der Genesis" = laws of original
  time-consciousness, reproduction, association, associative expectation
  (`:4495`). "ob also Genetisches dabei völlig auszuschalten ist" [whether the
  genetic can be wholly excluded] from static analysis is left a question
  (`:4497`). In static consideration apperceptions are "fertige" and have a
  "weit zurückliegende 'Geschichte'" (`:4509`). Closing remark: he must go
  through the *Ideen* again (`:4511`). Husserl's footnote: three
  phenomenologies: of universal structures, constitutive, of genesis
  (`:4519`–`:4522`). *Surprise:* the text ends in questions about order, not
  in a definition; and it distinguishes three things, not two.
- `:5261`–`:5335`, Beilage XIX (pp. 411–416; 12–14 Feb. 1926). "Voran gegeben
  und evident ist immer die 'fertige' Struktur der impressionalen Welt ... Wie
  kann man daraus eine ideale Genesis aus dem Chaos zeichnen?" [What is given
  first and evident is always the "finished" structure of the impressional
  world ... How can one draw from it an ideal genesis out of chaos?]
  (`:5300`–`:5303`). "genetische Urelemente, auf die der Abbau der
  Intentionalität und ihre Genesis zurückführt" [genetic primal elements to
  which the dismantling of intentionality and its genesis lead back]
  (`:5300`). "Solche Analysen, Konstruktionen aller hierher gehörigen idealen,
  abstraktiven Möglichkeiten müssen durchgeführt werden" [Such analyses,
  constructions of all the ideal, abstractive possibilities belonging here,
  must be carried out] (`:5317`). "Ein Fehler in den Vorlesungen" [a mistake in
  the lectures] (`:5319`). The born-blind case (`:5300`). *Surprise:* written
  days before the course ended, it names the method "Abbau" and
  "Konstruktion", and asks how a genesis can be had at all from a finished
  present.

## 2. Hua XIV, Beilage I (June 1921)

`husserl/husserl-hua-14-…-zweiter-teil.md:1172`–`:1302` (pp. 34–42), read in
full. Companion of the Hua XI treatise, same year. Three kinds of inquiry
(`:1263`): static (correlation of constituting consciousness and constituted
objectivity, genetic problems excluded); inquiries into "die Typik
verschiedener ... Gestalten des Erlebens und der Genese" [the types of shapes
of experience and of genesis] without the problems of individuality; the
phenomenology of monadic individuality, "einer zusammenhängenden Genese" [of a
connected genesis]. `:1261`: "Auch vereinzelte Genesen kann ich beschreiben
und Gesetze der Genesis, ohne das Problem der universellen Genesis einer
Monade ... in Angriff zu nehmen" [I can describe isolated geneses too, and
laws of genesis, without taking up the problem of the universal genesis of a
monad]. `:1265`: the systematic phenomenology "wie ich sie mir dachte" follows
the levels of constitution; these "sind also genetische Betrachtungen".
`:1289`: "Der Konstitution nachgehen ist nicht der Genesis nachgehen, die eben
Genesis der Konstitution ist" [to follow constitution is not to follow
genesis, which is precisely genesis of constitution]; static phenomenology as
"die Phänomenologie der Leitfäden"; correlation "ist keine Bedingtheit der
Genesis, es wird hier nicht das Bedingte aus dem Bedingenden". `:1291`: he
also describes statically "die Typik der Zusammenhänge im Bewusstsein irgend
einer Entwicklungsstufe", as in the *Ideen*. *Surprise:* a middle term
between static analysis and the history of a monad: laws of genesis and
isolated geneses. The lectures of Hua XI look like this middle kind.

## 3. Hua I, *Cartesianische Meditationen* §§ 37–38 (1929; published 1931 in French, 1950 in German)

`husserl/husserl-hua-1-…:2300`–`:2356` (pp. 109–113), read in full. § 37: time
as "formale Gesetzmäßigkeit einer universalen Genesis"; the ego constitutes
itself "in der Einheit einer Geschichte"; "Die zunächst ausgebildete
Phänomenologie ist bloß statische, ihre Deskriptionen sind analog den
naturhistorischen" [the phenomenology first developed is merely static; its
descriptions are analogous to those of natural history]; questions of
universal genesis "über die Zeitformung hinausgehend" remain far off; even
when raised they are bound to an ego for which a world is already
constituted. § 38: active and passive genesis; what meets us "sozusagen
fertig" as a mere thing; the synthesis has "ihre sich in ihr selbst
bekundende Geschichte" [its history, which announces itself in it]; "ohne uns
auf den Boden der Passivität zurückzuversetzen" [without putting ourselves
back on the ground of passivity] the meditating ego finds "intentionale
Verweisungen ..., die auf eine Geschichte führen"; "Urstiftung". *Surprise:*
the 1929 text says the history is read off the finished phenomenon by its
references, and that even genetic inquiry is first "in einer Bindung" to a
constituted world. This is later than the lectures (1920–26) and may not be
read back into them without the dates. § 39 (`:2357`–`:2461`) still to read.

## 4. Secondary works held

### Bernet, Kern and Marbach, *An Introduction to Husserlian Phenomenology*, ch. 7 "Static and Genetic Constitution"

`bernet/bernet-an-introduction-…-bernet-kern-marbach.md:2538`–`:2614`
(pp. 195–202), read in full as far as the file goes (it breaks off mid-chapter,
and footnotes 125–130 are empty; the corpus map says only chs. 1, 3 § 1, 4 and
6 are Bernet's, so the chapter is cited by the three names, not as Bernet).
Position: the distinction belongs to 1917–21; before it "genesis" in *Ideen*
is improper (kinetic view of constitution; the image of levels; time).
Properly genetic phenomenology asks after the *history* of finished
constitutive systems: habitualities acquired by the I, "the history of the
object itself" (quoting Hua XI 345). "Husserl did not elaborate clearly
enough the methodology of genetic, constitutional analysis" (`:2547`). The
status of time-consciousness is ambiguous: as mere form it "possesses no
becoming, no genesis" and is "the foundation of genetic phenomenology"
(`:2571`). Two forms of association (`:2595`). This is the received view the
study starts from: genetic = history of acquisitions, read from the 1921
treatise and CM. It quotes Hua XI only from the treatise (336 ff., 340, 345)
and from pp. 118 and 128 of the lectures.

### Bégout, *La Généalogie de la logique* (Vrin; dépôt légal June 2000, `:15`)

`begout/begout-la-genealogie-de-la-logique.md`. No page markers; cite by
chapter and section title.
- `:354`–`:471`, Introduction ch. II, "Phénoménologie statique et
  phénoménologie génétique : la révolution généalogique", read in full.
  Position: the distinction is never given a final statement by Husserl and
  its borders are porous; static survives after 1917–21 and genetic analyses
  precede it (with Steinbock). Static = "fondation de validité"
  (Geltungsfundierung), on late manuscripts; genetic = constitution as
  motivation; passive motivation founds "le valable ... non le valide".
  Genetic analysis thematises less genesis than "les structures d'essence de
  la genèse" (`:412`). The two are joined in a "généalogie": regressive
  archaeology from the constituted, progressive teleology from the original,
  in zigzag. Cites Welton (transition to genetic analysis as an immanent
  demand of static analysis).
- `:639`–`:686`, Part I ch. II, "L'analyse génétique de la passivité : le
  commencement et l'abstraction", read in full. Position: in the *Analysen*
  the first-in-itself is pre-affective and so never experienced; Husserl
  therefore uses "l'abstraction idéalisatrice", "des hypothèses, des
  conjectures" (`:651`), a necessary conjecture; the original is "construit à
  partir des éléments dérivés". The saliences (Abgehobenheiten) are the
  starting points; "Überschiebung" is the method that mirrors the phenomena
  (`:677`–`:683`). Bégout says Husserl elsewhere calls these constructions
  "substructions" (`:673`). *Check against Hua XI:* at `:2331` Husserl uses
  "unverständliche Substruktionen" for what his "methodisches Prinzip"
  *excludes*, and at `:2345` binds every motive of theory to the evidences of
  the finished present. Whether Husserl's abstraction is conjecture (Bégout)
  or the isolation of a dependent layer of what is evidently given is a point
  on which the primary text may narrow the nearest precedent. Bégout also
  dates the lectures "Leçons de 1921" throughout; the main text of §§ 16 ff.
  is the 1925/26 version (n. `:1266`).
- Still to read: `:687`–`:774` (association), `:992`–`:1037` ("Affection et
  formation d'unité ou le circus vitiosus de la passivité"), `:1194`–`:1287`
  (genesis of logic; archaeology; dismantling), `:1549`–`:1652` (error and
  the mixing of memories), `:1653`–`:1726` (Conclusion).

### Drummond, "On Welton on Husserl" (NYB III, 2003)

NYB III file `:3068`–`:3135`, §§ I–III read in full. Drummond reports Welton's
*The Other Husserl* (2000; not held): the static/genetic distinction as the
systematic expansion of method beyond its "Cartesian" formulation; ambiguity
whether genetic analysis deepens or replaces static analysis. Drummond's own
position: the dichotomy that matters is between "the rhetoric of the
phenomenological program and method" and "the actual phenomenological
descriptions"; the practice shapes the method, and the statements of method
"catch up" in the 1920s with analyses in place since 1907. He names Hua XI
only in passing (the determinable X as "a self-transforming identity").
*Use:* a precedent for reading the practice against the programme; he does
not carry it out for the genetic method of Hua XI. Welton is known here only
through Drummond's report.
