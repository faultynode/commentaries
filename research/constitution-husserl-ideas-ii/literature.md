## The received view and its holders

The received view: *Ideen II* constitutes reality in a stratified order
(material thing, animal body and soul, spirit), each layer founded on the one
below, so that the person is built on nature.

- **Heidegger, GA 20 § 13 d (1925).** Held; read (`:2560`–`:2620`). The
  sharpest statement: the "Fundamentalschicht" stays natural reality; the
  person is a "mehrschichtiges Weltding" assembled from layers. Level: full
  for the passage.
- **Husserl himself, *Ideen I* § 152 (1913).** Held. Material reality as the
  lowest level beneath all others. The programmatic source of the view.
- **Mattens (2006).** Held; § 1 read. Stratification as the paradigm of
  constitutive analysis, read from the phantom. Level: part.
- **Moran & Cohen, *Husserl Dictionary* (2012), "Constitution".** Held; read.
  The neo-Kantian formation of objects by acts. Level: full for the entry.

## Readings near the candidate directions (none read in full)

- **Ricœur, "Analyses et problèmes dans *Ideen II* de Husserl" (1951–52, two
  parts).** Index: `ricoeur-1952-analyses-et-problemes-dans-ideen` (part 2).
  Level: title. From memory: argues a tension in *Ideen II* between the
  idealism of constitution and a realism of the body and of persons. The
  nearest precedent for any thesis about a double order. Not held.
- **Merleau-Ponty, "Le philosophe et son ombre" (1959).** Not in the index
  as a record; cited in titles of Moran (2013) and others. Level: memory.
  Reads *Ideen II* as uncovering, beneath the stratified constitution, a
  pre-theoretical layer that the analysis presupposes and cannot constitute.
  Not held.
- **Sokolowski, *The Formation of Husserl's Concept of Constitution* (1964).**
  Index: review by Valdinoci (1978). Level: title. The standard account of
  the concept's development. Not held.
- **Nenon & Embree (eds.), *Issues in Husserl's Ideas II* (1996).** Index:
  review by Durie (2000). Level: title.
- **Lohmar, "On Some Motives for Husserl's Genetic Turn in his Research on a
  Foundation of the Geisteswissenschaften" (2018).** Level: abstract
  (`literature-candidates.md`). Bears on dating the genetic layer.
- **Luft, "Husserl's concept of the 'transcendental person'" (2005).** Level:
  abstract. Another look at Husserl–Heidegger through the person.
- **Burns (2014), Cobb-Stevens (1983), de la Cadena (2019, 2022), Caminada
  (2019, via a review), Costelloe (2003), Mingo (2021, on the priority of the
  personalistic attitude in the Heidegger–Husserl debate), Yoshimi (2010,
  psycho-physical laws).** Level: abstract or title.
- **Kern (2003) on Hua XXXII; Nenon (2012) on Hua Mat IV.** Reviews of the two
  *Natur und Geist* lecture courses (1927, 1919). Level: title. They establish
  that the editions exist.

## What this means for the thesis

No held secondary work argues about the composition layers of *Ideen II* in
relation to the order of constitution. Whether Ricœur or the Nenon–Embree
volume already does is the question to settle before a thesis is called new
(acquisitions 3 and 6).
