## Passage notes (before any thesis)

1. **Hua IV § 18 c, `:1494`–`:1501` (p. 65–66).** "Ursprünglich konstituiert sich die reale Welt stufenweise": sense-things under the space-form with the body as centre of orientation, then "Realisierung" into causal real things. *Surprise*: the lowest stratum is already bound to the body and kinaesthesis: the sense-thing is a unity "in" perceptions and "kinaesthetic constellations". The bottom layer is not mere nature; it is nature-for-a-body.
2. **Hua V, Beilage I, `:1713` (1912).** Restricting to theoretical experience, "Natur" and the body, and with it the soul, constitute themselves "in *Wechselbezogenheit aufeinander*, in eins miteinander". *Surprise*: the editor (Hua IV `:400`) says this mutual, simultaneous constitution "excludes every psychologistic theory of layers" and is stressed more in 1912 than in later versions. A claim about layers of composition made by the editor, testable in the held text.
3. **Hua IV § 64, `:4593`–`:4598` (pp. 297–98).** Nature is "ein Feld durchgängiger Relativitäten", relative to an absolute that bears them: spirit. "Streichen wir alle Geister aus der Welt, so ist keine Natur mehr." *Surprise*: the book that begins with material nature ends by making it relative to spirit; the order of exposition and the order of dependence run in opposite directions.
4. **Hua IV Beilage "Die Stufen der Konstitution der objektiven Wirklichkeit", `:5252`–`:5258` (pp. 324–25).** The stages "bauen sich … aufeinander" from the single ego; then "noch eine anders konstituierte Welt": the naturalistic apprehension as an "apperzeptive Umwandlung" of persons. Nature is "der in oder mittels Darstellung sich konstituierenden realen Einheiten"; spirit, of realities given by "absolute Bekundung". *Surprise*: two modes of givenness (presentation by adumbration vs. manifestation), not two layers, mark the difference of nature and spirit; and the naturalistic world is a *transformation* of the personal one, i.e. constituted from above.
5. **Hua IV § 49 e, `:3228`–`:3236` (pp. 180–82).** In the naturalistic attitude "Ich denke" is a "Naturfaktum, fundiert in dem Leibe"; physical nature is "die alle sonstige Natur begründende". *Surprise*: the founding vocabulary (fundiert, begründend) is placed explicitly inside one attitude.
6. **Hua IV apparatus, Husserl's marginalia in Landgrebe's typescript (1924–28), `:7270` ff.** On the first chapter: "Dieses Kapitel muß völlig neu ausgearbeitet werden"; later "Von hier an scheint alles besserungsbedürftig"; "Das hätte schon früher gesagt werden müssen. — Aber es wäre dann eine bloße Bestimmung derjenigen Subjektivität, in der es sich konstituiert." *Surprise*: the last layer judges the order of exposition, and the third remark names the problem of saying the constituting subjectivity first. Located: the first on ch. 1 (`:7270`); "besserungsbedürftig" on p. 74, ll. 20 f., and "früher gesagt" on p. 89, ll. 31 ff. (both `:7331`). The apparatus prints them in one long line; the page-marker check will not reach them.
7. ***Ideen I* § 151–152, recheck `:6807`–`:6829` (pp. 315–19 of 1913).** Stages and layers of thing-constitution; the intersubjective thing as "konstitutive Einheit höherer Ordnung"; and "Als unterste Stufe liegt schließlich die materielle Realität allen anderen Realitäten zugrunde", yet the founded is "neuartig", never a sum. *Surprise*: the programme already contains both the founding order and the non-reducibility; the 1913 text does not yet say spirit is absolute.
8. **GA 20 § 13 c–d, `:2493`–`:2620` (pp. 165–74).** Heidegger reports the priority of the personalistic attitude ("die naturalistische ist der personalistischen untergeordnet") and quotes § 64 (his n. 42–47: "Ideen II, S. 297–302"), then objects: "Die *Fundamentalschicht* bleibt das *Naturwirkliche*, daraufgebaut ist das Seelische und auf dieses das Geistige"; the person as "mehrschichtiges Weltding"; the guiding definition *animal rationale*. *Surprise*: he cites "§ 12 … § 15" for what are §§ 61–64 in print, so he read a version with another numbering (Landgrebe's? Stein's?), and he concedes the priority of the personal before denying it effect.
9. ***Sein und Zeit* § 10, n. (`:993`, p. 47 n.).** Names the three sections and says Husserl "beginnt seine Darlegungen" with a sentence on Dilthey. It is the opening of section 3, § 48 (Hua IV `:3170`, p. 173), but the wording differs: SZ "faßte zwar … methodisch richtigen Lösungen", print "erschaute zwar … methodisch sicheren Lösungen". Heidegger's paraphrase, or the wording of the version he read (Landgrebe's, 1924/25). *To check* in the apparatus for p. 173. (A first literal search for "Dilthey faßte zwar" failed; the holdings entry is corrected.)
10. **Mattens (2006), § 1.** The phantom shows that "each level in the stratification can be contrasted to both a lower and a higher level"; it is "the paragon of constitutive analysis". A held statement of the stratification reading as method.
11. **BKM, "Static and Genetic Constitution" (`:2538`–`:2550`).** Static phenomenology starts from species of stable objects; a properly genetic phenomenology is first outlined 1917–21. *Bearing*: the layers of *Ideen II* straddle that date.
13. **Read after the plan (2026-11-13).**
