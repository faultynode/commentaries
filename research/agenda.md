## constitution-husserl-ideas-ii-study (2026-11-13)

**Acquire** (all requested at the gate, none supplied; each is a declared limit in the study).
- Husserl, *Natur und Geist*, SS 1927 (Hua XXXII): what order of constitution Husserl taught during his last notes to *Ideen II*.
- Husserl, *Natur und Geist*, SS 1919 (Hua Mat IV): dates the underground doctrine and § 64 between Stein (c. 1918) and Landgrebe (1924/25).
- Ricœur, "Analyses et problèmes dans *Ideen II* de Husserl" (both parts): nearest precedent.
- Merleau-Ponty, "Le philosophe et son ombre": rival or precedent.
- Sokolowski, *The Formation of Husserl's Concept of Constitution*.
- A clean copy of the English *Ideas II* (CW3); Nenon & Embree (eds.), *Issues in Husserl's Ideas II*; Stein, *Zum Problem der Einfühlung*; Fink (1939); Dilthey (1894); Lohmar (2018) in full; Hua XXXVI.

**Held but unread, ranked high or flagged.**
- Hua XIII Beilage LVII ("wohl 1927"): the absolute as intersubjective interrelatedness; first test of the study's reading of the 1924–28 additions to § 64.
- Hua XIV (1921–28); Hua XV (1929–35, the highest-ranked file for constitution vocabulary).
- Hua XVI, *Ding und Raum* (1907), "Stufen der Raumkonstitution": antecedent of section 1's layers.

**Directions not taken.** The intersubjective sense of constitution; § 18's normality and anomaly; the § 63 footnote on the "Lebenswelt" (undated); Husserl's marginal note to Hua IV p. 202 ("hier haben mich immer Schiefheiten beirrt"); which arrangement numbered §§ 61–64 as §§ 12–15 (GA 20, 171); CM IV and Hua XI on static and genetic constitution.

**Write-back done.** New note `sources/notes/constitution-ideen-ii-layers.md` (dated layer table, pointers by §, English page concordance); corpus map: Hua IV dating tools and traps, CW3 damage, note listed.

## world-husserl-heidegger-study (2026-11-12)

**Acquire.** Each entry says what it would settle.
- Heidegger GA 29/30: whether "world-forming" continues the 1928 concept; the plurality of worlds.
- Heidegger GA 56/57 and the rest of the early Freiburg courses: dates the surrounding world and "to world" before 1925.
- Heidegger, "Der Ursprung des Kunstwerkes" (not found in the Holzwege file): whether world after 1929 can still be called a for-the-sake-of.
- Heidegger, *Was ist Metaphysik?*: the nothing in the same year as the *nihil originarium*.
- German GA 27.
- Husserl, *Erfahrung und Urteil* §§ 7–10, Hua XXXIX: whether "ground of validity" is a standing term or a late coinage.
- Husserl's marginalia to *Sein und Zeit*: what Husserl made of SZ 64 and 366.
- Critical apparatus of Hua VI: dates the two *Krisis* appendices. (~~Hua IV~~: corrected 2026-11-12 by the constitution study: the Hua IV apparatus, with a page-to-manuscript concordance, is held at `husserl-hua-4-…:7209`–end, and dates *Ideen II* §§ 11 and 50.)
- GA 29/30 and GA 56/57 are in `sources/pdf-input/` as unconverted PDFs (found 2026-11-12); they need converting, not acquiring.

**Held but unread** (the survey of 2026-11-12 ranks these high for
"world", and no note points to them).
- Hua XV (highest hit count in the archive), with Hua XIII and XIV: home-world and alien world; bears on the claim that Husserl's world is one.
- Hua VIII, the main text beyond the passages used.
- Hua XXVII, "Über die gegenwärtige Aufgabe der Philosophie".
- Hua IX, the later lectures and Beilagen.
- GA 18, GA 60, GA 61 (the early concept of world, in translation).

**Directions not taken.**
- Husserl's "motivation" as a third modality between validity and for-the-sake-of.
- The history of the concept of world in *Vom Wesen des Grundes* and GA 27 §§ 33–34, read whole.
- Time: the two-sided horizon of *Ideen I* § 27 against the ecstatic horizon of SZ § 69 c.
- The plural: Husserl's surrounding worlds (CM § 60) against Heidegger's we-world and own world (SZ 65).
- Scheler's "relativity of things to existence", which GA 26 names beside Husserl.

**Precedents not engaged** (found by `study_prep.py lit`; known by
title only; the index holds reviews of the two books, dated 2004 and
2007, and the books' own years below are from memory and unchecked).
- Lilian Alweiss, *The World Unclaimed: A Challenge to Heidegger's Critique of Husserl* (2003).
- Søren Overgaard, *Husserl and Heidegger on Being in the World* (2004).
- Sebastiano Galanti Grollo, "Rethinking Husserl's lifeworld" (*Continental Philosophy Review*, 2022).
